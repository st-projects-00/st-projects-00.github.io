Ibyo waguze

Kuraho amatangazo gikomeza guhuzwa na konti yawe ya Google Play, bityo ushobora kugikomeza kukibona ukoresheje Garura ibyaguzwe ku gice cya Konti. Iminsi isigaye ku Ikarita y'ukwezi ntishobora kongera kugarurwa kuri indi telefone biturutse ku nyandiko zacu z'ibyaguzwe. Iguma mu byabitswe kuri telefone ukoresha (kandi niwongera kwinjira nyuma, ibyo byabitswe byoherezwa mu kibitswe gishya cyo mu gicu). Ibindi byose waguze byongewe mu mukino wawe igihe wabiguraga.

Nyuma yo gusiba amakuru yawe yo mu gicu, ibyo waguze ntibiba bigihujwe nawe, kandi bikaguma bityo keretse wongeye kwinjira muri Play Games. Google Play ibika kopi yayo y'akamenyetso kuri buri cyatumijwe, kandi ntidushobora kugakuraho. Iyo umukino wongeye kugenzura icyaguzwe na seriveri yacu, seriveri yongera kwandika akamenyetso ka Google (ID ya konti yawe y'umukinnyi) kuri iyo nyandiko y'icyaguzwe, ariko ntiyongera guhuza iyo nyandiko nawe cyangwa ngo ikikugarurire. Ubusanzwe umukino ugenzura Kuraho amatangazo muri ubu buryo buri gihe utangiye. Niwongera kwinjira muri Play Games ukoresheje konti imwe, ibyaguzwe Google igishyiraho akamenyetso ka konti yawe kandi byongeye kugira ako kamenyetso ku nyandiko yacu bishobora kongera guhuzwa nawe. Mu by'ukuri ibyo ni Kuraho amatangazo: amakarita n'ibindi byaguzwe birakoreshwa iyo bitanzwe, bityo umukino ntubyongera kubigenzura, kandi iminsi isigaye ku ikarita ntiyongera guhuzwa nawe. Gusiba amakuru yawe yo mu gicu ntibizigera bibuza Kuraho amatangazo kugaruka biturutse kuri Google Play.

Amakuru ari kuri telefone yawe

Gusiba amakuru yo mu gicu ntibikora ku byabitswe kuri telefone yawe byerekana aho ugeze. Bikuraho kuri telefone ID yawe y'umukinnyi ya Play Games, izina rigaragara n'ikimenyetso cy'isomo, kandi kubika mu gicu bikomeza kuba bifunze kugeza wongeye kwinjira. Kugira ngo ukureho aho ugeze n'amakuru y'ibyaguzwe, kanda Siba aho ugeze ku gice cya Konti (niba winjiye, ibi kandi bisimbuza ibyo wabitse mu gicu intangiriro nshya). Kugira ngo ukureho ibintu byose umukino ubika kuri telefone yawe, harimo n'igenamiterere, siba ububiko bwa porogaramu mu igenamiterere rya Android cyangwa ukuremo porogaramu. Niba telefone yawe ibika kopi y'amakuru ya porogaramu kuri konti yawe ya Google, cyangwa yarayakuye kuri telefone ya kera mu gihe cyo kwimura igikoresho, ushobora kubicunga mu igenamiterere ryo kubika kopi rya telefone yawe.
