I-Petalwick ka-ST Projects · iphakheji com.stprojects.petalpop

I-Petalwick ayinawo ama-akhawunti ayo: ukuxhumana kwe-akhawunti okukodwa kuphela ukungena kwakho ku-Google Play Games, futhi leli khasi likubonisa ukuthi ungakusula kanjani lokho kuxhumana nedatha yakho. Idatha esiyigcina kuseva yethu exhunywe kuwe ngu-kulondoloziwe kwakho efwini nomaki ekuthengeni okwenzile, okukhona kuphela uma udlale ungene ku-Google Play Games. Ungakusula wena emdlalweni, noma usicele ukuthi sikususe nge-imeyili.

Inketho 1: emdlalweni (ngokushesha)

  1. Vula i-Petalwick bese uvula isitolo.
  2. Iya kuthebhu ye-I-akhawunti.
  3. Thepha u-Susa idatha yefu bese uqinisekisa.

Inkinobho ivela ngesikhathi ungene ekulondolozeni efwini. Uma ubona okuthi "Awungenile", qala uthephe u-Ngena. Udinga uxhumano lwe-inthanethi.

Inketho 2: nge-imeyili

Thumela i-imeyili ku-battlearenaunlimited@gmail.com enesihloko esithi "Petalwick data deletion". Faka i-Ikhodi yomdlali eboniswa kuthebhu ye-I-akhawunti ngesikhathi ungene (ibukeka njengokuthi 3FA9-0C21-7B4E), ukuze sithole idatha yakho. Uma ungakwazi ukuvula umdlalo, thumela inombolo ye-oda (iqala ngo-GPA.) evela kurisidi le-Google Play esikhundleni salokho. Sizophendula zingakapheli izinsuku ezingu-30, futhi sizoqinisekisa uma idatha yakho isisuliwe.

Ukuthenga kwakho

U-Susa izikhangiso uhlala exhunywe ne-akhawunti yakho ye-Google Play, ngakho usengakuthola futhi ngo-Buyisela ukuthenga kuthebhu ye-I-akhawunti. Izinsuku ezisele ku-Ikhadi lanyanga zonke azisakwazi ukubuyiswa efonini enye emarekhodini ethu okuthenga. Zihlala kokulondoloziwe kufoni oyisebenzisayo (futhi uma uphinde ungena kamuva, lokho okulondoloziwe kulayishwa kokulondoloziwe okusha efwini). Konke okunye okuthengile kwengezwa emdlalweni wakho lapho ukuthenga.

Ngemva kokusula idatha yakho yefu, ukuthenga kwakho akusaxhunyiwe kuwe, futhi kuhlala kunjalo ngaphandle kokuthi uphinde ungene ku-Play Games. I-Google Play igcina ikhophi yayo yomaki ku-oda ngalinye, futhi asikwazi ukuwususa. Uma umdlalo uphinde uhlola ukuthenga neseva yethu, iseva ibhala kabusha umaki we-Google (i-ID ye-akhawunti yakho yomdlali) kulelo rekhodi lokuthenga, kodwa ayilixhumi irekhodi kuwe noma ikubuyisele lona. Ngokuvamile umdlalo uhlola u-Susa izikhangiso ngale ndlela njalo uma uqala. Uma uphinde ungena ku-Play Games nge-akhawunti efanayo, ukuthenga i-Google esakumaka nge-akhawunti yakho futhi okuphinde kuphathe umaki erekhodini lethu kungaphinde kuxhunywe kuwe. Empeleni lokho ngu-Susa izikhangiso: amakhadi nokunye ukuthenga kuyasetshenziswa lapho kulethwa, ngakho umdlalo awukuhloli futhi, futhi izinsuku ezisele ekhadini aziphindi zixhunywe kuwe. Ukusula idatha yakho yefu akulokothi kumise u-Susa izikhangiso ukuthi abuye evela ku-Google Play.

Idatha esefonini yakho

Ukusula idatha yefu akuyithinti inqubekela phambili elondolozwe efonini yakho. Kususa efonini i-ID yakho yomdlali ye-Play Games, igama elibonisiwe nethokheni yeseshini, futhi ukulondoloza efwini kuhlala kuvaliwe uze uphinde ungene. Ukuze ususe inqubekela phambili yakho nolwazi lokuthenga, thepha u-Setha kabusha inqubekela phambili kuthebhu ye-I-akhawunti (uma ungene, lokhu kuphinde kufake esikhundleni sokulondoloziwe kwakho efwini isiqalo esisha). Ukuze ususe konke umdlalo okugcina efonini yakho, kuhlanganise nezilungiselelo, sula isitoreji se-app kuzilungiselelo ze-Android noma ukhiphe i-app. Uma ifoni yakho yenza isipele sedatha ye-app ku-akhawunti yakho ye-Google, noma iyikopishe efonini endala ngesikhathi sokudlulisela idivayisi, ungakuphatha lokho kuzilungiselelo zesipele zefoni yakho.

Idatha yezikhangiso

Ulwazi oluqoqwe yi-Google AdMob ukuze ibonise izikhangiso lugcinwa yi-Google, hhayi yithi. Ungasetha kabusha noma usule i-ID yakho yokukhangisa kuzilungiselelo zefoni yakho, futhi ufunde kabanzi kokuthi Indlela i-Google esebenzisa ngayo ulwazi oluvela kuma-app asebenzisa izinsizakalo zayo.
