I-Petalwick ingumdlalo wephazili wohlobo lokufanisa-3 we-Android, oshicilelwe yi-ST Projects ("thina"), unjiniyela ozimele ozinze e-United States. Le nqubomgomo ichaza ukuthi umdlalo uqoqa luphi ulwazi, kungani, nokuthi kwenzekani kulo. Isebenza ku-app ye-Android eshicilelwe ngaphansi kwegama lephakheji com.stprojects.petalpop.

Ngamafuphi: awudali i-akhawunti kithi. Inqubekela phambili yakho ilondolozwa efonini yakho. Umdlalo ubonisa izikhangiso ezivela ku-Google AdMob, futhi ukuthenga kwenziwa nge-Google Play. Uma ungene ku-Google Play Games, inqubekela phambili yakho iphinde ilondolozwe kuseva yethu ukuze ukwazi ukuyibuyisa efonini entsha. Ungayisusa leyo datha yefu emdlalweni noma nini. Ungavula le nqubomgomo emdlalweni ngenkinobho ethi Inqubomgomo yobumfihlo kuthebhu ye-I-akhawunti esitolo.

Ulwazi olugcinwa kudivayisi yakho

Umdlalo ulondoloza okulandelayo endaweni yokugcina ye-app uqobo efonini yakho:

  • Inqubekela phambili yomdlalo: izinga, izinkanyezi, ingadi yakho, izinhlamvu, amagugu, okuqinisayo (boosters), izimpilo, nenqubekela phambili yemicimbi ne-Iphasi Lengadi.
  • Izilungiselelo, njengetimu ekhanyayo noma emnyama.
  • Ulwazi lokuthenga: ukuthi uthengeni nokuthi nini (umlando wokuthenga oboniswa kuthebhu ye-I-akhawunti), isamba sakho sokusebenzisa imali ngenyanga, ama-ID ama-oda okuthenga okulethiwe (ukuze ukuthenga kungalokothi kunikezwe kabili), nempendulo yakho embuzweni othi "18 noma ngaphezulu" obuzwa ngaphambi kokuthenga kokuqala.
  • Uma usebenzisa ukulondoloza efwini: i-ID yakho yomdlali ye-Google Play Games negama elibonisiwe (elibonakala njengokuthi "ungene njengo-"), kanye nethokheni yeseshini ekugcina uxhumene neseva yethu.

Le datha ihlala efonini yakho ngaphandle kokuthi usebenzise ukulondoloza efwini (ngezansi). I-app ivumela Ukwenza Isipele Okuzenzakalelayo (Auto Backup) kwe-Android, ngakho i-Android ingayifaka esipeleni sedivayisi yakho ku-akhawunti yakho ye-Google, noma iyikopishele efonini entsha ngesikhathi sokudlulisela idivayisi, kuya ngezilungiselelo zefoni yakho. Leso sipele siphathwa yi-Google, futhi asikwazi ukusibona.

Umdlalo awulokothi ukucele imvume futhi awukwazi ukufinyelela endaweni yakho, koxhumana nabo, ezithombeni, kukhamera, kumakrofoni noma emafayilini akho. Usebenzisa ukufinyelela ku-inthanethi nesimo senethiwekhi, i-Google Play Billing, nezimvume ze-ID yokukhangisa ezidingwa yi-SDK yezikhangiso ye-Google (kuhlanganise nezimvume zezinsizakalo zokukhangisa ze-Android). Imitapo yolwazi ye-Google iphinde ifake izimvume zokuvusa (wake-lock) nezensizakalo yangaphambili (foreground service) yemisebenzi yangemuva. Umdlalo awunayo imitapo yolwazi yokuhlaziya noma yokubika ukuphahlazeka eyethu.

Izikhangiso (Google AdMob)

I-Petalwick ibonisa izikhangiso ezinikezwa yi-Google AdMob:

  • Amavidiyo omvuzo, okhetha ukuwabuka ukuze uthole umvuzo emdlalweni, isibonelo impilo eyengeziwe. Ahlale engaphoqelekile futhi akhawulelwe ku-8 ngosuku.
  • Izikhangiso eziphakathi (interstitial), eziboniswa phakathi kwamazinga (ubuningi esisodwa emazingeni angu-3 ngamunye). Uma uthenga u-Susa izikhangiso, izikhangiso eziphakathi ziyama. Amavidiyo omvuzo ahlala etholakala uma uwafuna.

Ukuze kuboniswe izikhangiso, zilinganiswe futhi kuvinjelwe ukukhwabanisa kwezikhangiso, i-Google Mobile Ads SDK ese-app iqoqa ulwazi ngqo kudivayisi yakho futhi ilithumele ku-Google. Lokhu kungafaka i-ID yokukhangisa yedivayisi yakho, ikheli lakho le-IP (okungatholwa kulo indawo ecishe ibe yiyo), ulwazi mayelana nedivayisi yakho ne-app, nokusebenzelana kwakho nezikhangiso. Asilutholi lolu lwazi ngendlela ekuhlonzayo. Indlela i-Google elusebenzisa ngayo ichazwe kokuthi Indlela i-Google esebenzisa ngayo ulwazi oluvela kumasayithi noma kuma-app asebenzisa izinsizakalo zayo naku-Nqubomgomo Yobumfihlo ye-Google.

Ukukhetha kwakho izikhangiso. Uma umdlalo uqala, usebenzisa ithuluzi lemvume le-Google (i-User Messaging Platform) ukuhlola ukuthi imvume iyadingeka yini lapho ukhona. Lapho ithuluzi lemvume le-Google likudinga (isibonelo e-European Economic Area, e-United Kingdom nase-Switzerland), uboniswa umlayezo wemvume we-Google futhi ungakhetha ukuthi izikhangiso zenziwe ngokwakho yini. Ungakushintsha ukukhetha kwakho kamuva ngenkinobho ethi Ukukhetha ubumfihlo bezikhangiso kuthebhu ye-I-akhawunti esitolo, evela lapho ithuluzi lemvume le-Google likudinga. Kunoma iyiphi ifoni ye-Android ungaphinda usethe kabusha noma usule i-ID yakho yokukhangisa kuzilungiselelo zefoni yakho (ngokuvamile Izilungiselelo > Ubumfihlo > Izikhangiso, noma Izilungiselelo > Google > Izikhangiso).

Ukuthenga (Google Play Billing)

Ukuthenga ngaphakathi kwe-app kuphathwa yi-Google Play Billing. Ukhokhela i-Google, futhi imigomo yokukhokha nenqubomgomo yobumfihlo ye-Google kuyasebenza enkokhelweni. Asilokothi sibone noma sigcine inombolo yekhadi lakho, imininingwane yasebhange, ikheli lokukhokha noma i-imeyili ye-akhawunti yakho ye-Google.

Uma uthenga okuthile, i-app ithumela imininingwane yokuthenga evela ku-Google Play (i-ID yomkhiqizo, ithokheni yokuthenga ne-ID ye-oda) kuseva yethu yokuqinisekisa. Iseva ihlola ne-Google Play ukuthi ukuthenga kuyiqiniso, akukaze kusetshenziswe ngaphambili futhi akubuyiselwanga imali, bese kuthi kuphela lapho into inikezwe. Sigcina irekhodi lokuthenga ngakunye (umkhiqizo, i-ID ye-oda, isikhathi sokuthenga nesimo, njengokubuyiselwa imali) ukuze siqinisekise ukuthenga, sivimbele ukukhwabanisa, siphathe ukubuyiselwa kwemali futhi sihlangabezane nezibopho zentela nezomthetho. Irekhodi lifakwa ngaphansi kwe-hash yendlela eyodwa yethokheni yokuthenga. Ithokheni uqobo ayigcinwa: besiyogcina kuphela ukubhalisela (subscriptions), futhi umdlalo awuthengisi nokukodwa. I-Google iphinde yazise iseva yethu uma ukuthenga kubuyiselwe imali noma kukhanseliwe.

Uma ungene ku-Google Play Games lapho uthenga, umdlalo umaka ukuthenga nge-ID ye-akhawunti yakho yomdlali: i-hash yendlela eyodwa yegama lephakheji le-app ne-ID yakho yomdlali ye-Play Games, hhayi i-ID uqobo. Umaki uya ku-Google Play ne-oda (njenge-"ID ye-akhawunti efihliwe" ye-Google) nakuseva yethu nokuhlolwa kokuthenga. Ikuvumela ukuthi uphinde uthole lokhu efonini enye engene ku-akhawunti efanayo ye-Play Games:

  • Susa izikhangiso, okungaphelelwa yisikhathi. Kuphinde kuxhunywe ne-akhawunti yakho ye-Google Play, ngakho ungakubuyisa ngaphandle kokungena ku-Play Games: thepha u-Buyisela ukuthenga kuthebhu ye-I-akhawunti.
  • Izinsuku ezisele ku-Ikhadi lanyanga zonke olithenge ungene.

I-Ikhadi lanyanga zonke aliyona into evulwa unomphela: ngalinye lisebenza izinsuku ezingu-30 kusukela osukwini olithenge ngalo. Uma uthenga elilodwa ngesikhathi elinye lisasebenza, umdlalo wengeza izinsuku ezintsha ezingu-30 ngemva kwezinsuku ezisele kuwe. Iseva yethu ibala izinsuku ezisele ngendlela efanayo: idlula emakhadini owathenga ungene, kusukela kwelidala kakhulu, futhi ngalinye lengeza izinsuku ezingu-30 kusukela osukwini lokuthengwa kwalo noma ekupheleni kwezinsuku ezisasele, noma yikuphi okwenzeka kamuva. Ngakho ukubuyisela efonini enye kubuyisa zonke izinsuku ezisele, kuhlanganise namakhadi angqwabene. Uma umdlalo ukucela ukuthi ukhethe phakathi kwenqubekela phambili esefonini yakho nokulondoloziwe kwakho efwini, u-Susa izikhangiso, nezinsuku ezisele ku-Ikhadi lanyanga zonke elithengwe ungene, kuyabuyiswa noma ngabe ukhetha yikuphi.

Konke okunye okuthengayo (amagugu, amaphakheji, amaqoqo okuqinisayo ne-Premium ye-Iphasi Lengadi) kwengezwa emdlalweni wakho lapho ukuthenga. Akubuyiswa emarekhodini ethu okuthenga, futhi kubuya efonini entsha kuphela njengengxenye yokulondoloziwe kwakho efwini.

Ukuthenga ngemali yangempela kungokwabadlali abaneminyaka engu-18 noma ngaphezulu. Umdlalo uyabuza ngaphambi kokuthenga kwakho kokuqala futhi uvala ukuthenga kubadlali abathi bancane. Umdlalo uphinde ukhawulele ukuthenga ngemali yangempela ku-US$100 ngenyanga yekhalenda. Ugcina isamba esiqhubekayo efonini yakho, njengengxenye yokulondoloziwe komdlalo wakho, futhi umkhawulo usethwa kabusha mhla lu-1 enyangeni ngayinye (UTC).

Ulwazi olwabelwana nezinkampani zangaphandle

I-Google AdMob iqoqa ulwazi ngqo nge-app ukuze ibonise izikhangiso, njengoba kuchaziwe ngenhla.

I-Google Play iphatha izinkokhelo nokungena ku-Play Games, futhi ithola umaki we-ID ye-akhawunti yomdlali kuma-oda owenzayo ungene. Iseva yethu ixhumana ne-Google Play ukuze iqinisekise ukuthenga nokungena kwakho.

I-Google Cloud ibamba iseva yethu (Cloud Run) nesizindalwazi (Firestore). I-Google icubungula le datha egameni lethu.

Asiluthengisi ulwazi lwakho, futhi asabelani ngalo nanoma ubani omunye, ngaphandle uma umthetho ukudinga.

Lapho ulwazi lwakho lugcinwa khona

Iseva yethu nesizindalwazi kusebenza ku-Google Cloud esifundeni i-us-central1, e-United States. Umdlalo unikezwa nge-Google Play emazweni amaningi. Noma ngabe uwusebenzisa kuphi, ulwazi oluchazwe ngaphansi kokuthi "Ukuthenga" nokuthi "Ukulondoloza efwini" luthunyelwa futhi lucutshungulwe e-United States, lapho imithetho yokuvikela idatha ingahluka kuleyo yalapho ukhona. Noma ngabe uhlala kuphi, le nqubomgomo isebenza kuwe, futhi isigaba esithi "Amalungelo akho" sichaza amalungelo engeziwe abantu kwezinye izindawo abanawo ngaphansi komthetho wendawo.

Indlela ulwazi lwakho oluvikelwa ngayo

Konke ukuxhumana phakathi kwe-app neseva yethu kubethelwa ngesikhathi sokudluliswa (HTTPS). Iseshini yakho neseva yethu ixhunywe kule app nakwi-ID yakho yomdlali, futhi iphelelwa yisikhathi ngemva kwezinsuku ezingu-30. Iseva ikhawulela inani lezicelo eziwamukelayo, ukuvimbela ukusetshenziswa kabi.

Amalogi eseva yethu aqopha isikhathi, uhlobo lwesicelo nomphumela waso, ukuthi sathatha isikhathi esingakanani, nekheli le-IP esivela kulo. Amalogi ezicelo ze-Google Cloud aphinde aqophe i-user agent yedivayisi yakho (imodeli yefoni yakho, inguqulo ye-Android nenguqulo yenjini yesiphequluli). Ekuhlolweni kokuthenga amalogi afaka i-ID yomkhiqizo ne-ID ye-oda, futhi izaziso zokubuyiselwa kwemali ezivela ku-Google ziloga i-ID ye-oda. Ukusisiza silandelele izinkinga, eminye imigqa yelogi ifaka izinhlamvu zokuqala ezingu-12 ze-ID ye-akhawunti yakho yomdlali (ezifana ne-Ikhodi yomdlali yakho) noma ze-hash yethokheni yokuthenga. Amalogi awalokothi aqukethe okulondoloziwe kwakho, i-ID yakho yomdlali ephelele noma i-ID ye-akhawunti yomdlali, noma noma yimaphi amathokheni. Siwasebenzisela ukuphepha, ukukhawulela ukusetshenziswa kabi nokulungisa izinkinga. I-Google Cloud iwagcina izinsuku ezingu-30 ngokuzenzakalelayo.

Silugcina isikhathi esingakanani

  • Kudivayisi yakho: inqubekela phambili yomdlalo nolwazi lokuthenga kuze uthephe u-Setha kabusha inqubekela phambili (okuphinda kufake esikhundleni sokulondoloziwe kwakho efwini isiqalo esisha uma ungene). I-ID yakho yomdlali ye-Play Games, igama elibonisiwe nethokheni yeseshini kuze uthephe u-Susa idatha yefu, usule idatha ye-app noma ukhiphe i-app. Izilungiselelo ezifana netimu kuze usule idatha ye-app noma ukhiphe i-app.
  • Okulondoloziwe efwini nezinguqulo zakho zangaphambili: kuze ukususe. Ukulondoloza okusha ngakunye kufaka esikhundleni sokwangaphambili, futhi kugcinwa izinguqulo zangaphambili ezingadluli kwezingu-2. Okwamanje akukho ukusulwa okuzenzakalelayo kokulondoloziwe okungasebenzi. Uma singakwethula, sizokusho lapha.
  • Amarekhodi okuthenga nama-oda: agcinwa iminyaka engu-7 ngemva kokuthenga, ngenxa yentela, ukubuyiselwa kwemali nokuvimbela ukukhwabanisa. Bese kuthi umsebenzi wokuhlanza wansuku zonke usule irekhodi ngalinye, kanye nesimangalo salo se-oda nemicimbi yalo yokubuyiselwa kwemali noma yokukhansela. Uma usula idatha yakho yefu, i-ID ye-akhawunti yakho yomdlali isuswa kuwo ngokushesha (bheka "Ukusula idatha yakho" ukuze wazi ukuthi umaki we-oda we-Google ubhalwa kabusha nini), futhi okusele kwerekhodi ngalinye kugcinwa kuze kube yileso sikhathi seminyaka engu-7.
  • Amalogi eseva: izinsuku ezingu-30 ngokuzenzakalelayo, bese esulwa ngokuzenzakalelayo yi-Google Cloud.
  • Izipele zesizindalwazi: siphinde sigcine amakhophi abethelwe esizindalwazi sethu sonke ku-Google Cloud: umlando wokubuyisela isizindalwazi kunoma yisiphi isikhathi ezinsukwini ezingu-7 ezedlule, izipele zansuku zonke ezigcinwa izinsuku ezingu-7 nezipele zamaviki onke ezigcinwa amaviki angu-14. Sizisebenzisa kuphela ukululama ekulahlekelweni yidatha noma ukubuyisela inqubekela phambili yomdlali uma ecela. Izipele azikwazi ukushintshwa, ngakho idatha oyisulayo (kuhlanganise ne-ID ye-akhawunti yakho yomdlali esusiwe emarekhodini okuthenga) ihlala kuzo kuze kuphele isikhathi sazo: kuze kube amaviki angu-14 ngemva kokuyisula.

Ukusula idatha yakho

Emdlalweni: vula isitolo, uye kuthebhu ye-I-akhawunti bese uthepha u-Susa idatha yefu. Lokhu kusula ngokushesha esizindalwazini sethu esisebenzayo okulondoloziwe kwakho efwini, izinguqulo zakho zangaphambili nerekhodi elixhuma i-ID yakho yomdlali ye-Play Games nedatha yakho, futhi kususa i-ID ye-akhawunti yakho yomdlali emarekhodini akho okuthenga. Amarekhodi okuthenga uqobo agcinwa kuze kube iminyaka engu-7 ngemva kokuthenga ngakunye, njengoba kuchaziwe ngenhla, ngaphandle kwe-ID ye-akhawunti yakho yomdlali (ngokukodwa okuhlukile, okuchazwe ngezansi). Inqubekela phambili esefonini yakho iyagcinwa. Idatha esusiwe ingahlala ezipeleni zethu ezibethelwe, esizisebenzisa kuphela ukululama ekulahlekelweni yidatha noma ukubuyisela inqubekela phambili yomdlali uma ecela, kuze kuphele isikhathi sazo: kuze kube amaviki angu-14.

Lokhu kusho ukuthini ekuthengeni: u-Susa izikhangiso uhlala exhunywe ne-akhawunti yakho ye-Google Play, ngakho usengakubuyisa ngo-Buyisela ukuthenga. Izinsuku ezisele ku-Ikhadi lanyanga zonke azisakwazi ukubuyiswa efonini enye emarekhodini ethu okuthenga. Zihlala kokulondoloziwe kufoni oyisebenzisayo (futhi uma uphinde ungena kamuva, lokho okulondoloziwe kulayishwa kokulondoloziwe okusha efwini).

Ngemva kokusula idatha yakho yefu, ukuthenga kwakho akusaxhunyiwe kuwe, futhi kuhlala kunjalo ngaphandle kokuthi uphinde ungene ku-Play Games. I-Google Play igcina ikhophi yayo yomaki ku-oda ngalinye, futhi asikwazi ukuwususa. Uma umdlalo uphinde uhlola ukuthenga neseva yethu, iseva ibhala kabusha umaki we-Google (i-ID ye-akhawunti yakho yomdlali) kulelo rekhodi lokuthenga, kodwa ayilixhumi irekhodi kuwe noma ikubuyisele lona. Ngokuvamile umdlalo uhlola u-Susa izikhangiso ngale ndlela njalo uma uqala. Uma uphinde ungena ku-Play Games nge-akhawunti efanayo, ukuthenga i-Google esakumaka nge-akhawunti yakho futhi okuphinde kuphathe umaki erekhodini lethu kungaphinde kuxhunywe kuwe. Empeleni lokho ngu-Susa izikhangiso: amakhadi nokunye ukuthenga kuyasetshenziswa lapho kulethwa, ngakho umdlalo awukuhloli futhi, futhi izinsuku ezisele ekhadini aziphindi zixhunywe kuwe. Ukusula idatha yakho yefu akulokothi kumise u-Susa izikhangiso ukuthi abuye evela ku-Google Play.

Nge-imeyili: ungaphinde usicele ukuthi sisule idatha yakho ngokuthumela i-imeyili ku-battlearenaunlimited@gmail.com. Sicela usho ukuthi imayelana ne-Petalwick futhi ufake i-Ikhodi yomdlali yakho evela kuthebhu ye-I-akhawunti. Uma ungakwazi ukuvula umdlalo, thumela inombolo ye-oda (iqala ngo-GPA.) evela kurisidi le-Google Play esikhundleni salokho. Sizophendula zingakapheli izinsuku ezingu-30, futhi sizoqinisekisa uma idatha yakho isisuliwe.

Ukuze ususe inqubekela phambili yakho nolwazi lokuthenga efonini yakho, thepha u-Setha kabusha inqubekela phambili kuthebhu ye-I-akhawunti (uma ungene, lokhu kuphinde kufake esikhundleni sokulondoloziwe kwakho efwini isiqalo esisha). Ukuze ususe konke umdlalo okugcina efonini yakho, kuhlanganise ne-ID yakho yomdlali ye-Play Games, igama elibonisiwe, ithokheni yeseshini nezilungiselelo, sula idatha ye-app kuzilungiselelo ze-Android noma ukhiphe i-app. Izinyathelo eziphelele zisekhasini lokusula idatha.

Amalungelo akho

Uma use-EEA, e-UK noma e-Switzerland, umthetho wokuvikela idatha (i-GDPR noma i-UK GDPR) ukunika ilungelo lokufinyelela kudatha yomuntu siqu esiyigcinile ngawe, uyilungise, isulwe, ukhawulele noma uphikisane nendlela esiyisebenzisa ngayo, futhi uthole ikhophi yayo. Asiluthengisi ulwazi lomuntu siqu. Ngaphandle kolwazi i-AdMob SDK ye-Google eluqoqayo ukuze ibonise izikhangiso, njengoba kuchaziwe ngenhla, asabelani ngolwazi lomuntu siqu lokukhangisa ngokuziphatha okweqa izimo (cross-context). Ungakhawulela ukwenziwa kwezikhangiso ngokwakho ngokusetha kabusha noma ngokusula i-ID yakho yokukhangisa kuzilungiselelo zefoni yakho, nangenkinobho ethi Ukukhetha ubumfihlo bezikhangiso kuthebhu ye-I-akhawunti, evela lapho ithuluzi lemvume le-Google likudinga. Ngeke sikuphathe ngokuhlukile ngokusebenzisa la malungelo.

Ukuze usebenzise noma yiliphi lala malungelo, sithumelele i-imeyili ekhelini elingezansi futhi ufake i-Ikhodi yomdlali eboniswa kuthebhu ye-I-akhawunti, ukuze sithole idatha yakho. Sizophendula zingakapheli izinsuku ezingu-30.
