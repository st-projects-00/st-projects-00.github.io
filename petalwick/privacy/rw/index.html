Ibyaguzwe (Google Play Billing)

Ibigurwa muri porogaramu bicungwa na Google Play Billing. Wishyura Google, kandi amabwiriza yo kwishyura na politiki y'ibanga bya Google ni byo bikurikizwa ku kwishyura. Ntitwigera tubona cyangwa tubika nimero y'ikarita yawe, amakuru ya banki yawe, aderesi yo kwishyuriraho cyangwa imeyili ya konti yawe ya Google.

Iyo uguze ikintu, porogaramu yohereza amakuru y'icyaguzwe aturuka kuri Google Play (ID y'igicuruzwa, ikimenyetso cy'icyaguzwe na ID y'icyatumijwe) kuri seriveri yacu igenzura. Seriveri igenzura na Google Play ko icyaguzwe ari icy'ukuri, kitigeze gikoreshwa mbere kandi kitasubijwe amafaranga, nyuma ni bwo ikintu gitangwa. Tubika inyandiko ya buri cyaguzwe (igicuruzwa, ID y'icyatumijwe, igihe cyaguriwe n'uko gihagaze, nk'icyasubijwe amafaranga) kugira ngo twemeze ibyaguzwe, twirinde uburiganya, ducunge isubizwa ry'amafaranga kandi twuzuze inshingano z'imisoro n'iz'amategeko. Inyandiko ibikwa munsi ya hash y'icyerekezo kimwe y'ikimenyetso cy'icyaguzwe. Ikimenyetso ubwacyo ntikibikwa: twakibika gusa ku bufatabuguzi (subscriptions), kandi umukino nta na bumwe ugurisha. Google kandi imenyesha seriveri yacu iyo icyaguzwe gisubijwe amafaranga cyangwa gihagaritswe.

Niba winjiye muri Google Play Games igihe ugura, umukino ushyira ku cyaguzwe akamenyetso ka ID ya konti yawe y'umukinnyi: hash y'icyerekezo kimwe y'izina rya paki ya porogaramu na ID yawe y'umukinnyi ya Play Games, si ID ubwayo. Ako kamenyetso kajya kuri Google Play hamwe n'icyatumijwe (nka "ID ya konti yahishwe" ya Google) no kuri seriveri yacu hamwe n'igenzura ry'icyaguzwe. Gatuma ushobora kongera kubona ibi bikurikira kuri indi telefone yinjiye muri konti imwe ya Play Games:

  • Kuraho amatangazo, kitarangira na rimwe. Gihujwe kandi na konti yawe ya Google Play, bityo ushobora kukigarura utinjiye muri Play Games: kanda Garura ibyaguzwe ku gice cya Konti.
  • Iminsi isigaye ku Ikarita y'ukwezi waguze winjiye.

Ikarita y'ukwezi si ugufungura guhoraho: buri imwe imara iminsi 30 uhereye ku munsi wayiguriyeho. Uguze imwe indi ikiri gukora, umukino wongeraho iminsi 30 mishya nyuma y'iminsi igusigaranye. Seriveri yacu ibara iminsi isigaye mu buryo bumwe: inyura ku makarita waguze winjiye, uhereye ku ya kera kurusha izindi, kandi buri imwe yongeraho iminsi 30 uhereye ku itariki yaguriweho cyangwa ku iherezo ry'iminsi igisigaye, icyaza nyuma muri byo. Bityo kugarura kuri indi telefone bigarura iminsi yose isigaye, harimo n'amakarita yagerekeranyijwe. Niba umukino ugusabye guhitamo hagati y'aho ugeze kuri telefone yawe n'ibyo wabitse mu gicu, Kuraho amatangazo n'iminsi isigaye ku Ikarita y'ukwezi yaguzwe winjiye bigarurwa uko wahitamo kose.

Ibindi byose ugura (diyama, amapaki, amapaki y'ibyongerera imbaraga na Premium ya Pasi y'Ubusitani) byongerwa mu mukino wawe igihe ubiguze. Ntibigarurwa biturutse ku nyandiko zacu z'ibyaguzwe, kandi bigaruka kuri telefone nshya gusa nk'igice cy'ibyo wabitse mu gicu.

Kugura n'amafaranga nyayo bigenewe abakinnyi bafite imyaka 18 cyangwa irenga. Umukino ubaza mbere yo kugura bwa mbere kandi ufunga kugura ku bakinnyi bavuga ko bakiri bato. Umukino kandi ugabanya kugura n'amafaranga nyayo kuri US$100 mu kwezi kw'ingengabihe. Ubika igiteranyo kigezweho kuri telefone yawe, nk'igice cy'ibyo umukino wawe wabitse, kandi urugero rusubirwamo ku itariki ya 1 ya buri kwezi (UTC).

Amakuru dusangiza abandi

Google AdMob ikusanya amakuru inyuze muri porogaramu kugira ngo yerekane amatangazo, nk'uko byasobanuwe haruguru.

Google Play icunga kwishyura no kwinjira muri Play Games, kandi yakira akamenyetso ka ID ya konti y'umukinnyi ku byo utumiza winjiye. Seriveri yacu ivugana na Google Play kugira ngo igenzure ibyaguzwe no kwinjira kwawe.

Google Cloud icumbikira seriveri yacu (Cloud Run) n'ububiko bw'amakuru (Firestore). Google itunganya aya makuru mu izina ryacu.

Ntitugurisha amakuru yawe, kandi ntituyasangiza undi uwo ari we wese, keretse aho amategeko abisaba.

Aho amakuru yawe abikwa

Seriveri yacu n'ububiko bw'amakuru bikorera kuri Google Cloud mu karere ka us-central1, muri Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika. Umukino utangwa binyuze muri Google Play mu bihugu byinshi. Aho waba uwukoreshereza hose, amakuru asobanurwa munsi ya "Ibyaguzwe" na "Kubika mu gicu" yoherezwa kandi agatunganyirizwa muri Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika, aho amategeko arengera amakuru ashobora gutandukana n'ay'aho uri. Aho waba utuye hose, iyi politiki irakureba, kandi igice "Uburenganzira bwawe" gisobanura uburenganzira bw'inyongera abantu bo hamwe na hamwe bafite hakurikijwe amategeko y'aho batuye.

Uko amakuru yawe arindwa

Itumanaho ryose hagati ya porogaramu na seriveri yacu rihishwa mu gihe cyo kohereza (HTTPS). Isomo ryawe na seriveri yacu rihujwe n'iyi porogaramu na ID yawe y'umukinnyi, kandi rirangira nyuma y'iminsi 30. Seriveri igabanya umubare w'ubusabe yakira, kugira ngo hirindwe ikoreshwa nabi.

Inyandiko z'ibikorwa (logs) za seriveri yacu zandika igihe, ubwoko bw'ubusabe n'ibyavuyemo, igihe bwatwaye, na aderesi ya IP bwaturutseho. Inyandiko z'ubusabe za Google Cloud zandika kandi user agent y'igikoresho cyawe (ubwoko bwa telefone yawe, verisiyo ya Android na verisiyo ya moteri ya mushakisha). Ku igenzura ry'ibyaguzwe, inyandiko zirimo ID y'igicuruzwa na ID y'icyatumijwe, kandi amatangazo ya Google yerekeye isubizwa ry'amafaranga yandika ID y'icyatumijwe. Kugira ngo zidufashe gukurikirana ibibazo, imirongo imwe y'inyandiko irimo inyuguti 12 za mbere za ID ya konti yawe y'umukinnyi (zimwe na Kode y'umukinnyi yawe) cyangwa za hash y'ikimenyetso cy'icyaguzwe. Inyandiko ntizigera zirimo ibyo wabitse, ID yawe y'umukinnyi yuzuye cyangwa ID ya konti y'umukinnyi, cyangwa ibimenyetso ibyo ari byo byose. Tuzikoresha mu by'umutekano, kugabanya ikoreshwa nabi no gukemura ibibazo. Google Cloud izibika iminsi 30 mu buryo busanzwe.

Uburenganzira bwawe

Niba uri muri EEA, mu Bwongereza cyangwa mu Busuwisi, amategeko arengera amakuru (GDPR cyangwa UK GDPR) aguha uburenganzira bwo kubona amakuru bwite tukubikiye, kuyakosora, kuyasibisha, kugabanya cyangwa kwanga uko tuyakoresha, no kubona kopi yayo. Ushobora kandi gutanga ikirego ku rwego rushinzwe kurengera amakuru iwanyu. Dukoresha amakuru yawe kugira ngo tuguhe umukino no kubika mu gicu wasabye, ku nyungu zacu zemewe n'amategeko zo kwirinda uburiganya no kurinda umutekano wa serivisi, kugira ngo twuzuze inshingano z'amategeko nko kubika inyandiko z'imisoro, kandi, ku matangazo ajyanishwa nawe, dushingiye ku ruhushya utanga mu butumwa bw'uruhushya bwa Google.

Niba uri muri California cyangwa muri indi leta ya Amerika ifite itegeko ry'ibanga, ufite uburenganzira bwo kumenya amakuru bwite dukusanya, kuyasibisha cyangwa kuyakosoza, no kwanga ko agurishwa cyangwa asangizwa. Ntitugurisha amakuru bwite. Uretse amakuru SDK ya AdMob ya Google ikusanya kugira ngo yerekane amatangazo, nk'uko byasobanuwe haruguru, ntidusangiza amakuru bwite ku matangazo ashingiye ku myitwarire mu bihe bitandukanye (cross-context). Ushobora kugabanya kujyanisha amatangazo nawe usubiramo cyangwa usiba ID yawe y'amatangazo mu igenamiterere rya telefone yawe, no ukoresheje buto ya Amahitamo y'ibanga ry'amatangazo iri ku gice cya Konti, igaragara aho igikoresho cy'uruhushya cya Google kibisaba. Ntituzagufata mu buryo butandukanye kubera gukoresha ubu burenganzira.

Kugira ngo ukoreshe ubwo ari bwo bwose muri ubu burenganzira, twandikire imeyili kuri aderesi iri hasi kandi ushyiremo Kode y'umukinnyi igaragara ku gice cya Konti, kugira ngo tubone amakuru yawe. Tuzagusubiza mu minsi 30.
