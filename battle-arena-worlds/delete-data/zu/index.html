Battle Arena Worlds ka-ST Projects · iphakheji com.stprojects.battlearenaworlds

I-Battle Arena Worlds ayinakho ukubhalisa. Uma umdlalo uqala, ukungenisa ku-Epic Online Services ngokusebenzisa i-akhawunti yomdlali engaziwa exhunywe efonini yakho, futhi iwalethi yakho yesitolo (amakhoyini, amagugu nezinto zokubukeka onazo) igcinwa kuseva yethu ngaphansi kwe-ID yomdlali yaleyo akhawunti. Uma ukhethe ukungena nge-Google, i-Epic ibuye ixhumanise i-akhawunti yakho ye-Google naleyo ID yomdlali. Leli khasi lichaza indlela yokwenza ukuthi leyo datha isuswe.

Susa emdlalweni (kunconywa)

  1. Vula i-Battle Arena Worlds bese uxhuma ku-inthanethi.
  2. Iya kuthebhu ye-Izilungiselelo. Ngaphansi kwe-I-akhawunti, thepha u-Susa idatha yami.
  3. Qinisekisa kabili. Uma ukuthenga kusaqinisekiswa, umdlalo uzokuxwayisa kuqala.

Ukususa kwenzeka ngokushesha. Bese umdlalo uqala i-akhawunti yomdlali entsha engenalutho efonini yakho. Uma ukhiphe umdlalo kodwa ubungene nge-Google, usengakwenza lokhu: wufake kabusha, ungene nge-akhawunti efanayo ye-Google ku-Izilungiselelo, bese uthepha u-Susa idatha yami.

Noma usicele nge-imeyili

Uma ungakwazi ukusebenzisa umdlalo, thumela i-imeyili ku-battlearenaunlimited@gmail.com enesihloko esithi "Battle Arena Worlds data deletion" bese ufaka okukodwa kulokhu:

  • ikhodi yakho yomdlali, eboniswa emdlalweni ngaphansi kwe-Izilungiselelo > I-akhawunti (yithephe ukuze uyikopishe); noma
  • inombolo ye-oda (iqala ngo-GPA.) kusuka kurisidi ye-Google Play yanoma yikuphi ukuthenga kwamagugu noma kwephakethe lokuqala okwenzile emdlalweni. Ungayithola ku-imeyili yerisidi evela ku-Google Play, noma ohlelweni lwe-Play Store ngaphansi kokuthi Izinkokhelo nokubhaliselwe > Isabelomali nomlando. Amarekhodi ethu okuthenga axhumanisa leyo oda ne-ID yakho yomdlali, futhi yileyo ndlela esithola ngayo iwalethi yakho.

Sizophendula phakathi nezinsuku ezingu-30, futhi sizoqinisekisa lapho idatha yakho isisusiwe. Uma siyisusa nge-imeyili, ifoni yakho isagcina i-ID yomdlali efanayo, ngakho umdlalo uzovula iwalethi entsha engenalutho ngaphansi kwayo ngokuzayo lapho uqala. Ukuvimbela lokho, sula idatha yomdlalo noma uwukhiphe, ngaphambi noma ngemva kokusibhalela.

Uma ungakaze uthenge lutho futhi ungasenawo umdlalo, iwalethi yakho ifakwe kuphela ngaphansi kwe-ID yomdlali engahleliwe engenalo igama, i-imeyili noma imininingwane yokuxhumana, futhi asinayo indlela yokuyikhomba.

Amakhoyini, amagugu nezinto zokubukeka ezisusiwe, kuhlanganise nalezo ezithengwe ngamagugu, azikwazi ukubuyiswa. Uma udlala futhi, uqala ngewalethi engenalutho. I-Google Play igcina amarekhodi ayo ezinkokhelo zakho, kuhlanganise nethegi ye-akhawunti (i-hash yendlela eyodwa ye-ID yakho yomdlali) kuyi-oda ngalinye, futhi asikwazi ukuwasusa.

Idatha ephethwe yi-Epic

I-Epic Games, ehlinzeka ukungena nemidlalo eku-inthanethi, iphethe irekhodi le-ID yakho yomdlali: i-ID yedivayisi engaziwa, igama lomdlali elakhiwe (isibonelo Player4821), uhlobo nemodeli yefoni yakho, futhi, uma ungene nge-Google, ukuxhumana ne-akhawunti yakho ye-Google. Ukususa emdlalweni kubuye kususe ukuxhumana ne-Google ne-ID yedivayisi ku-Epic, ukuze kungabikho muntu ongangena kuleyo ID yomdlali futhi. I-Epic igcina okunye okusele kurekhodi ngaphansi kwenqubomgomo yobumfihlo ye-Epic Games. Ukususa idatha yakho nge-imeyili akulishintshi irekhodi le-Epic.

Idatha efonini yakho

Ukususa emdlalweni kubuye kususe amafayela esitolo efonini yakho (ikhophi yewalethi yakho, ukuthenga okulinde ukuthunyelwa, impendulo yakho embuzweni weminyaka nesamba semali esetshenziswe kule nyanga), irekhodi lokungena kwakho nge-Google ne-ID yedivayisi ye-Epic. Izilungiselelo zakho nezinga lakho kuhlala efonini. Akukaze kuthunyelwe kuseva yethu.

Ukuze ususe konke umdlalo okugcina efonini yakho, sula indawo yokugcina yohlelo ezilungiselelweni ze-Android (Izilungiselelo > Ama-app > Battle Arena Worlds > Isitoreji > Sula isitoreji) noma ukhiphe uhlelo. Lokhu kukodwa akuyisusi iwalethi kuseva yethu. Uhlelo lokusebenza luvala isipele se-Android nokudlulisela kwenye idivayisi, ngakho i-Android ayigcini ikhophi yale datha esipeleni se-akhawunti yakho ye-Google futhi ayiyidluliseli efonini entsha.
