Delete your Battle Arena Worlds data

Battle Arena Worlds by ST Projects · package com.stprojects.battlearenaworlds

Battle Arena Worlds has no sign-up. When the game starts, it signs you in to Epic Online Services with an anonymous player account tied to your phone, and your shop wallet (coins, gems and the cosmetics you own) is kept on our server under that account's player ID. This page explains how to have that data deleted.

How to request deletion: by email

The game does not yet have a delete button, and it does not yet show your player ID. To ask us to delete your data, email battlearenaunlimited@gmail.com with the subject "Battle Arena Worlds data deletion" and include:

  • The order number (it starts with GPA.) from the Google Play receipt of any gem or starter pack purchase you made in the game. You can find it in the receipt email from Google Play, or in the Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions > Budget & history. Our purchase records link that order to your player ID, which is how we find your wallet.

We will reply within 30 days, and we will confirm when your data has been deleted. Your player ID itself lives on your phone, so after we delete your wallet the game will open a new, empty wallet under the same player ID the next time it starts. To stop that, clear the game's data or uninstall it, before or after you write to us.

If you have never bought anything: your wallet is filed only under a random player ID and holds no name, email or contact details, and without a purchase we currently have no way to identify it. The deletion available to you is clearing the game's data on your phone or uninstalling the game (see "Data on your phone" below), which removes the device ID and detaches your phone from the account (unless Android restores the app's data from a backup). You are still welcome to email us.

What is deleted, what is kept, and for how long

Deleted coins, gems and cosmetics, including those bought with gems, cannot be restored. Google Play keeps its own records of your payments, including the account tag (a one-way hash of your player ID) on each order, and we cannot remove them.

Data held by Epic

Epic Games, which provides the sign-in and online matches, holds a record for your player ID: the anonymous device ID, the generated player name (for example Player4821) and your phone's make and model. Epic keeps it under the Epic Games Privacy Policy. Deleting your data with us does not delete it.

Data on your phone

To remove everything the game keeps on your phone (settings, level, your player name, the copy of your wallet, your answer to the age question, the Epic device ID and the small performance file kept for each session), clear the app's storage in Android settings (Settings > Apps > Battle Arena Worlds > Storage > Clear storage) or uninstall the app. This does not delete the wallet on our server. If your phone backs up app data to your Google account, or copied it from an old phone, you can manage that in your phone's backup settings.
