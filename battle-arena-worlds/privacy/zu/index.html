Isebenza kusukela zi-9 kuOkthoba 2026

I-Battle Arena Worlds iwumdlalo we-battle royale wabadlali abaningi we-Android, oshicilelwa yi-ST Projects ("thina"), umthuthukisi ozimele ozinze e-United States. Le nqubomgomo ichaza ukuthi umdlalo uqoqa luphi ulwazi, kungani, nokuthi kwenzekani kulo. Isebenza ohlelweni lokusebenza lwe-Android olushicilelwe ngaphansi kwegama lephakheji elithi com.stprojects.battlearenaworlds.

Ngamafuphi: awudingi ukubhalisa noma ukungena ngemvume. Uma umdlalo uqala, ukungenisa ku-Epic Online Services ngemuva ngokusebenzisa i-akhawunti yomdlali engaziwa exhunywe efonini yakho, hhayi egameni lakho noma ku-imeyili yakho. Uma uthanda, ungangena nge-Google ukuze i-akhawunti iqhubeke ikhona ngemva kokufaka kabusha noma efonini entsha. Leyo akhawunti isetshenziselwa imidlalo eku-inthanethi kanye newalethi yakho yesitolo (amakhoyini, amagugu nezinto zokubukeka onazo), esiyigcina kuseva yethu. Ukuthenga kwenziwa nge-Google Play. Umdlalo awunazo izikhangiso, ingxoxo, izwi, futhi awunazo izinsiza zethu zokuhlaziya noma zokubika ukuphahlazeka, futhi awukwazi ukufinyelela endaweni yakho, koxhumana nabo, ezithombeni, kukhamera, kumakrofoni noma kumafayela akho.

I-akhawunti yakho yomdlali (Epic Online Services)

Izici eziku-inthanethi zisebenzisa i-Epic Online Services (EOS), ehlinzekwa yi-Epic Games. Umdlalo awukaze ukucele i-akhawunti ye-Epic, ikheli le-imeyili noma iphasiwedi. Esikhundleni salokho, uma uqala, ungena ngemvume ngokusebenzisa i-ID yedivayisi engaziwa:

  • Lapho uqala ukuvula umdlalo, i-SDK ye-Epic esemdlalweni yakha i-ID yedivayisi yalokhu kufakwa futhi iyigcine endaweni yokugcina yohlelo efonini yakho. Uma ikwenza lokho, ithumela ku-Epic uhlobo nemodeli yefoni yakho, i-Epic ezisebenzisela ukuhlola izinkinga.
  • Bese i-Epic inika umdlalo i-ID yomdlali (i-Epic iyibiza ngokuthi i-ProductUserId): ikhodi engahleliwe enezinhlamvu ezingu-32 ekhomba i-akhawunti yakho kulo mdlalo. Ayixhunyiwe nanoma iyiphi i-akhawunti ye-Epic noma ikheli le-imeyili, futhi ixhunywa ne-akhawunti ye-Google kuphela uma ukhetha ukungena nge-Google (bheka ngezansi).
  • Umdlalo ukwakhela igama lomdlali, elithi "Player" lilandelwe izinombolo ezine ezingahleliwe (isibonelo Player4821), futhi ulithumela ku-Epic lapho ungena ngemvume. Awukwazi ukulishintsha, futhi alikaze libe negama lakho langempela.

I-Epic icubungula lolu lwazi ukuze ihlinzeke ukungena ngemvume, ukufanisa abadlali nokuxhumana phakathi kwabadlali, ngaphansi kwenqubomgomo yobumfihlo ye-Epic Games.

Ukungena nge-Google okungaphoqelekile

Kuthebhu ye-Izilungiselelo yomdlalo ungathepha u-Ngena nge-Google. Akuphoqelekile: umdlalo, imidlalo eku-inthanethi nesitolo konke kuyasebenza ngaphandle kwakho. Inhloso yakho kuphela ukugcina i-akhawunti yakho yomdlali, ngakho-ke newalethi yakho, uma ufaka umdlalo kabusha, usula idatha yawo noma uthuthela efonini entsha.

  • I-Android ibonisa ishidi lokungena le-Google. Okokuqala, i-Google ikutshela ukuthi izokwabelana ngegama lakho, ikheli le-imeyili nesithombe sephrofayela nohlelo. Bese i-Google inika umdlalo ithokheni ye-ID ye-Google esayiniwe.
  • Umdlalo uthumela leyo thokheni ku-Epic kuphela. I-Epic iyayihlola futhi ixhumanise i-ID ye-akhawunti yakho ye-Google ne-ID yakho yomdlali. Umdlalo awulifundi futhi awaligcini igama lakho, ikheli le-imeyili noma isithombe, futhi iseva yethu ayikaze ithole ithokheni noma imininingwane ye-akhawunti yakho ye-Google.
  • Ngemva kokufaka kabusha noma efonini entsha, ukungena nge-akhawunti efanayo ye-Google kubuyisa i-ID yomdlali efanayo newalethi efanayo. Uma ifoni isivele inenqubekela phambili ngaphansi kwenye i-ID yomdlali, umdlalo ubuza ukuthi iyiphi inqubekela phambili okufanele igcinwe. I-Epic isusa i-ID yomdlali ongayigcini; iwalethi yayo ihlala kuseva yethu, lapho kungekho muntu ongangena kuyo, kuze kube yilapho usicela ukuthi siyisuse.
  • Umdlalo ugcina ifayela elincane efonini yakho elibhala ukuthi i-akhawunti yakho ye-Google ixhunywe kuyiphi i-ID yomdlali.
  • U-Phuma ugcina ukuxhumana ne-Google futhi ubuyisela ifoni ku-akhawunti entsha yesivakashi engenalutho. Ukungena futhi kubuyisa i-akhawunti yakho.

Ithebhu ye-Izilungiselelo ibuye ibonise ikhodi yomdlali yakho: izinhlamvu zokuqala ezingu-12 ze-ID yakho yomdlali, ongazikopisha futhi uzisho lapho uxhumana nathi.

Imidlalo eku-inthanethi

Uma uthepha ukuze udlale ku-inthanethi, umdlalo ufuna igumbi (i-lobby ye-EOS) labadlali abafika ku-10. Ngesikhathi usegumbini, umdlalo ushicilela le mininingwane ukuze imidlalo yabanye abadlali iyifunde:

  • I-ID yakho yomdlali negama lomdlali, neqhawe olikhethile.
  • Imininingwane yobuchwepheshe esetshenziselwa ukukhetha ukuthi ifoni yomdlali ongubani ezosingatha umdlalo: inkumbulo efonini yakho, izinga layo lokusebenza kwezithombe, ukuthi ise-Wi-Fi noma kudatha yeselula, ukuthi ukonga ibhethri kuvuliwe yini, nezikhathi ze-ping zakho kwabanye abadlali.
  • Inguqulo yomdlalo, nesimo sokuxhumana kwakho nomdlalo.

Ifoni yomdlali oyedwa isingatha umdlalo ngamunye eku-inthanethi, futhi amanye amafoni axhuma kuyo ngensiza ye-peer-to-peer ye-Epic. Uma ukuxhumana okuqondile kungenzeka, amafoni asemdlalweni ashintshana ngethrafikhi yenethiwekhi ngqo, ngakho ngalinye lingabona amakheli e-IP amanye. Uma kungenjalo, ithrafikhi idlula kumaseva okudlulisa e-Epic. Ngesikhathi somdlalo, umdlalo womsingathi uthola ukunyakaza kwakho, iqhawe lakho, izinto zokubukeka ozigqokile nemiphumela yakho yomdlalo, futhi udlulisela amakhophi esimo somdlalo kumafoni abanye abadlali ukuze umdlalo uqhubeke uma umsingathi ehamba. Abanye abadlali babona igama lakho lomdlali ohlwini lwabakhishiwe nasemiphumeleni.

Umdlalo awunayo ingxoxo yombhalo futhi awunayo ingxoxo yezwi, futhi awunalo uhlu lwabangane, izimemo noma isimo sokuba khona.

Ukuthenga (Google Play Billing)

Amagugu nephakethe lokuqala kuthengwa nge-Google Play Billing. Ukhokha i-Google, futhi imigomo yokukhokha ye-Google kanye neNqubomgomo Yobumfihlo ye-Google kusebenza ekukhokheni. Asikaze sibone noma sigcine inombolo yekhadi lakho, imininingwane yasebhange, ikheli lokukhokha noma i-imeyili ye-akhawunti yakho ye-Google. Umdlalo ubuye ubuze i-Google Play ukuthi i-akhawunti yakho ye-Play ikuliphi izwe; impendulo ihlala efonini yakho futhi ayithunyelwa kithi.

Uma uqala ukuthenga, umdlalo unika i-Google Play i-hash yendlela eyodwa (MD5) ye-ID yakho yomdlali njengethegi ye-akhawunti ye-oda (i-"ID ye-akhawunti efihliwe" ye-Google). Ngemva kokuba ukhokhile, umdlalo uthumela umkhiqizo nethokheni yokuthenga ye-Google Play kuseva yethu. Iseva icela i-Google Play ukuthi iqinisekise ukuthi ukuthenga kungokwangempela, akukaze kusetshenziswe ngaphambili futhi akubuyiselwanga imali, bese kuphela yengeza amagugu kuwalethi yakho. Sigcina irekhodi lokuthenga ngakunye ukuze sikwazi ukuqinisekisa ukuthenga, sivimbele ukukhwabanisa, siphathe ukubuyiselwa kwemali futhi sifeze izibopho zentela nezomthetho:

  • Umkhiqizo, inombolo ye-oda ye-Google Play, isikhathi sokuthenga, ukuthi bekungukuthenga kokuhlola yini, nesimo sakho (isibonelo kubuyiselwe imali, nosuku nesizathu i-Google esinikezayo).
  • I-ID yakho yomdlali, nethegi ye-akhawunti i-Google Play eyibikayo ye-oda (i-hash ye-ID yakho yomdlali).

Irekhodi lifakwa ngaphansi kwe-hash yendlela eyodwa (SHA-256) yethokheni yokuthenga. Ithokheni ngokwayo ayigcinwa. I-Google ibuye yazise iseva yethu lapho ukuthenga kwenziwa, kubuyiselwa imali noma kukhanselwa, futhi iseva yethu icela i-Google uhlu lokuthenga okubuyiselwe imali izikhathi eziningana ngosuku. Iseva yethu igcina ilogi yalezo zaziso, efakwe ngaphansi kwe-hash efanayo nenombolo ye-oda.

Iminyaka nomkhawulo wokusebenzisa imali. Ukuthenga ngemali yangempela kungokwabadlali abaneminyaka engu-18 noma ngaphezulu. Ngaphambi kokuthenga kwakho kokuqala ngemali yangempela umdlalo ubuza ukuthi uneminyaka engu-18 noma ngaphezulu yini. Uma uphendula ukuthi ungaphansi kweminyaka engu-18, ukuthenga ngemali yangempela kuyavalwa kuleyo foni (amakhoyini asaqhubeka esebenza). Impendulo yakho igcinwa efonini yakho kuphela. Ukuthenga ngemali yangempela kubuye kukhawulelwe ku-US$200 ngenyanga yekhalenda (UTC). Umdlalo uhlola umkhawulo efonini yakho, futhi iseva yethu iwuhlola ku-akhawunti yakho, ibala ukuthenga ngakunye ngentengo yohlu yase-US, ngaphambi kokuba isikrini sokuthenga se-Google Play sivuleke.

Isitolo asinawo ama-loot box noma izinto ezikhokhelwayo ezingahleliwe.

Ulwazi olugcinwe efonini yakho

Umdlalo ugcina okulandelayo endaweni yokugcina yohlelo ngokwalo efonini yakho:

  • Izilungiselelo: iqhawe olikhethile, ukuzwela kwezilawuli, ukudubula okuzenzakalelayo nokuthepha ukuze wakhe, inani lamabhothi, amazinga omsindo nekhwalithi yezithombe, nezinga lezithombe umdlalo elifunde ukuthi lifanele ifoni yakho.
  • Izinga lakho, amaphuzu akho okuhlangenwe nakho, nendawo yakho yokugcina nababakhiphile.
  • Igama lakho lomdlali elakhiwe, ne-ID yedivayisi ye-Epic (egcinwa yi-SDK ye-Epic). Uma ungene nge-Google, ukuthi i-akhawunti yakho ye-Google ixhunywe kuyiphi i-ID yomdlali.
  • Ikhophi yewalethi yakho njengoba iseva yethu yagcina ukuyithumela, ukuze isitolo sisebenze ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi; imiphumela yemidlalo nokuthenga okulinde ukuthunyelwa kuseva yethu; impendulo yakho embuzweni weminyaka; nesamba semali esetshenziswe kule nyanga.
  • I-ID engahleliwe yalokhu kufakwa, esetshenziswa kuphela yisitolo efonini ngaphambi kokuba ukungena ku-Epic kuqedwe. Ayikaze ithunyelwe kuseva yethu.
  • Ngesikhathi usemdlalweni eku-inthanethi, inothi elifushane legumbi nomdlalo (ukuze umdlalo ukwazi ukujoyina futhi uma uvaleka). Lisuswa lapho umdlalo uphela.
  • Ifayela elilodwa elincane lokusebenza ngeseshini ngayinye (izinga lama-frame lomdlalo nezilungiselelo zezithombe), eligcinwa efonini yakho futhi elingakaze lilayishwe.

Uhlelo luvumela i-Auto Backup ye-Android, ngakho i-Android ingafaka le datha kusipele sedivayisi yakho ku-akhawunti yakho ye-Google, noma iyikopishele efonini entsha, kuya ngezilungiselelo zefoni yakho. Leso sipele siphathwa yi-Google, futhi asikwazi ukusibona.

Izimvume

Umdlalo awukaze ukucele imvume. I-manifest yawo ye-Android imemezela kuphela izimvume i-Android ezinikeza ngaphandle kokubuza:

  • Ukufinyelela ku-inthanethi, nokubuka isimo senethiwekhi ne-Wi-Fi (ukuze udlale ku-inthanethi, nokwazi ukuthi use-Wi-Fi noma kudatha yeselula).
  • I-Google Play Billing, nokuhlolwa kwelayisensi kwe-Google Play.
  • Ukudlidliza, ukugcina ifoni ivulekile, nokushintsha izilungiselelo zomsindo (kusetshenziswa yinjini yomdlalo).
  • Insiza yangaphambili (ukuvumelanisa idatha), nokuqala ngemva kokuba ifoni iqale kabusha: kusetshenziswa imitapo elanda futhi isethe idatha yomdlalo kusuka ku-Google Play, nangumtapo wemisebenzi yangemuva we-Android.
  • Imvume eyodwa yohlelo ngokwalo, ephethwe wuhlelo kuphela, esetshenziswa imitapo ye-Android engaphakathi kwalo.

Umdlalo awuceli izimvume zendawo, zoxhumana nabo, zekhamera, zemakrofoni, zezithombe noma zendawo yokugcina, futhi awukwazi ukufinyelela kuzo. Ukungena nge-Google okungaphoqelekile kusebenzisa i-Credential Manager ye-Android nomtapo wokungena we-Google, futhi kusebenza kuphela lapho uthepha u-Ngena nge-Google (noma, uma usungenile, ukuhlola ukuxhumana lapho kudingeka). Iphakheji yohlelo ibuye ifake imitapo ye-Google Play services injini yomdlalo eyengeza ngokuzenzakalelayo: umtapo omdala we-Google Sign-In ne-Play Games Services (ezizilungiselela lapho uhlelo luqala; asisethanga umdlalo we-Play Games, ngakho akukho ukungena noma iphrofayela ye-Play Games eyakhiwayo), nemitapo yendawo, ye-Drive neye-FIDO okungekho lutho emdlalweni oluyibizayo. Ngaphandle kwezimvume ezifanele, umtapo wendawo awukwazi ukufinyelela endaweni yakho.

I-Battle Arena Worlds ayibonisi zikhangiso futhi ayinayo i-SDK yokukhangisa. Ayinazo izinsiza zethu zokuhlaziya noma zokubika ukuphahlazeka, futhi ayikulandeleli kwezinye izinhlelo noma amawebhusayithi.

Ulwazi olwabelwana nezinkampani zangaphandle

I-Epic Games (Epic Online Services) ihlinzeka ukungena okungaziwa, amagumbi nokuxhumana phakathi kwabadlali, futhi ithola ulwazi oluchazwe ngaphansi kokuthi "I-akhawunti yakho yomdlali" nokuthi "Imidlalo eku-inthanethi". Uma ungena nge-Google, i-Epic ibuye ithole ithokheni yakho ye-ID ye-Google futhi igcine ukuxhumana phakathi kwe-akhawunti yakho ye-Google ne-ID yakho yomdlali. Inqubomgomo yobumfihlo ye-Epic Games.

I-Google ihlinzeka ukungena nge-Google okungaphoqelekile, ngakho uma ukusebenzisa, i-Google iyazi ukuthi ungene ku-Battle Arena Worlds. I-Google Play iphatha izinkokhelo futhi ithola ithegi ye-akhawunti eyenziwe i-hash kuma-oda akho. Iseva yethu ixhumana ne-Google Play ukuze iqinisekise ukuthenga. Inqubomgomo Yobumfihlo ye-Google.

I-Google Cloud isingatha iseva yethu nesizindalwazi sethu. I-Google icubungula le datha egameni lethu. Isaziso Sobumfihlo se-Google Cloud.

Abanye abadlali emidlalweni yakho eku-inthanethi babona igama lakho lomdlali, iqhawe lakho nezinto zokubukeka ozigqokile, futhi amafoni abo athola imininingwane yomdlalo echazwe ngaphansi kokuthi "Imidlalo eku-inthanethi".

Asiludayisi ulwazi lwakho, futhi asabelani ngalo nanoma ubani omunye, ngaphandle uma umthetho udinga lokho.

Lapho ulwazi lwakho lugcinwa khona

Iseva yethu isebenza ku-Google Cloud e-us-central1 (United States). Umdlalo unikezwa nge-Google Play emazweni amaningi. Noma ngabe uwusebenzisa kuphi, ulwazi oluchazwe ngaphansi kokuthi "Iwalethi yakho yesitolo" nokuthi "Ukuthenga" luthunyelwa kuseva yethu e-United States, lapho imithetho yokuvikela idatha ingahluka kweyalapho ukhona. I-Epic icubungula ulwazi eluthola njengoba kuchazwe kunqubomgomo yayo yobumfihlo.

Indlela ulwazi lwakho oluvikelwa ngayo

Konke ukuxhumana phakathi kohlelo neseva yethu kubethelwe ngesikhathi kudluliswa (HTTPS). Iseva yethu ivula iseshini ye-ID yomdlali kuphela ngethokheni yokungena esayinwe yi-Epic, futhi amaseshini ayo axhunywe kulolu hlelo naku-ID yakho yomdlali futhi aphelelwa yisikhathi ngemva kwezinsuku ezingu-30. Ikhawulela ukuthi yamukela izicelo ezingaki, ukuze kuvinjelwe ukuhlukumeza.

Amalogi eseva yethu abhala isikhathi, uhlobo lwesicelo nomphumela waso, ukuthi sathatha isikhathi esingakanani, nekheli le-IP esivela kulo. Amalogi ezicelo e-Google Cloud abuye abhale ikheli eliphelele lesicelo ne-user agent yedivayisi yakho (engafaka inguqulo yohlelo neye-Android). Eminye imigqa yelogi ifaka izinhlamvu zokuqala ezingu-12 ze-ID yakho yomdlali, umkhiqizo, inombolo ye-oda ye-Google Play, izinto ozithenga ngamakhoyini noma ngamagugu, amakhoyini afakwe ngemva komdlalo, noma izinhlamvu zokuqala ezingu-12 ze-hash yethokheni yokuthenga. Amalogi awakaze abe ne-ID yakho yomdlali ephelele, amathokheni akho okungena noma eseshini, noma amathokheni okuthenga. Siwasebenzisela ukuphepha, ukukhawulela ukuhlukumeza nokulungisa izinkinga. I-Google Cloud iwagcina cishe izinsuku ezingu-30 ngokuzenzakalelayo.

Sikugcina isikhathi esingakanani

  • Efonini yakho: kuze kube yilapho usula idatha yohlelo noma ukhipha uhlelo. Inothi lokujoyina futhi lisuswa lapho umdlalo ngamunye uphela.
  • Iwalethi yakho, namarekhodi emidlalo nokuthenga osekufakwe kuwe: inqobo nje uma i-akhawunti yakho isekhona, kuze kube yilapho uwasusa emdlalweni noma usicela nge-imeyili. Okwamanje akukho ukususwa okuzenzakalelayo kwamawalethi angasebenzi. Uma singakwethula, sizokusho lapha.
  • Amarekhodi okuthenga nama-oda: agcinwa iminyaka engu-7 ngemva kokuthenga, ngenxa yentela, ukubuyiselwa kwemali nokuvimbela ukukhwabanisa. Bese umsebenzi wokuhlanza wansuku zonke usula irekhodi ngalinye, nerekhodi lalo le-oda nezaziso zalo zokubuyiselwa kwemali noma zokukhansela.
  • Amalogi eseva: cishe izinsuku ezingu-30 ngokuzenzakalelayo, bese esuswa ngokuzenzakalelayo yi-Google Cloud.
  • Epic Online Services: kugcinwa yi-Epic ngaphansi kwenqubomgomo yayo yobumfihlo. Ukuxhumana ne-Google kugcinwa kuze kube yilapho usula idatha yakho emdlalweni.

Ukususa idatha yakho

Emdlalweni: kuthebhu ye-Izilungiselelo, ngaphansi kwe-I-akhawunti, thepha u-Susa idatha yami bese uqinisekisa kabili (udinga ukuxhumana ne-inthanethi). Kususa ngokushesha:
