Politiki y'ibanga ya Battle Arena Worlds

Iyi ni inyandiko yahinduwe mu rundi rurimi. Niba itandukanye na verisiyo y'Icyongereza, verisiyo y'Icyongereza ni yo yubahirizwa.

Itangira gukurikizwa ku wa 9 Ukwakira 2026

Battle Arena Worlds ni umukino wa battle royale ukinwa n'abantu benshi kuri Android, washyizwe ahagaragara na ST Projects ("twe"), ukora porogaramu wigenga ufite icyicaro muri Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika. Iyi politiki isobanura amakuru umukino ukusanya, impamvu, n'uko akoreshwa. Ireba porogaramu ya Android yashyizwe ahagaragara ku izina rya paki com.stprojects.battlearenaworlds.

Muri make: ntukeneye kwiyandikisha cyangwa kwinjira. Iyo umukino utangiye, ukwinjiza muri Epic Online Services inyuma utabibona, ukoresheje konti y'umukinnyi itazwi nyirayo, ifitanye isano na telefoni yawe, aho kuba izina ryawe cyangwa imeri yawe. Niba ubishaka, ushobora kwinjira ukoresheje Google kugira ngo konti igumeho nubwo wakongera gushyiramo umukino cyangwa ukabona telefoni nshya. Iyo konti ikoreshwa mu mikino yo kuri murandasi no mu ikofi yawe y'iduka (ibiceri, amabuye y'agaciro n'imitako ufite), tubika kuri seriveri yacu. Kugura binyura kuri Google Play. Umukino nta matangazo, nta kiganiro cyanditse, nta majwi ufite, kandi nta serivisi zacu bwite zo gusesengura cyangwa gutanga raporo z'ibibazo ufite, kandi ntushobora kugera aho uherereye, ku bo muvugana, ku mafoto, kuri kamera, kuri mikoro cyangwa ku madosiye yawe.

Konti yawe y'umukinnyi (Epic Online Services)

Ibikorwa byo kuri murandasi bikoresha Epic Online Services (EOS), itangwa na Epic Games. Umukino ntujya ugusaba konti ya Epic, aderesi ya imeri cyangwa ijambo ry'ibanga. Ahubwo, iyo utangiye, winjira ukoresheje ID y'igikoresho itazwi nyirayo:

  • Igihe ufunguye umukino bwa mbere, Epic SDK iri mu mukino ikora ID y'igikoresho y'iri shyirwamo ikayibika mu bubiko bwa porogaramu kuri telefoni yawe. Iyo ibikora, yoherereza Epic ikirango n'ubwoko bwa telefoni yawe, Epic ikabikoresha mu gusuzuma ibibazo.
  • Hanyuma Epic iha umukino ID y'umukinnyi (Epic iyita ProductUserId): kode y'inyuguti 32 zatoranyijwe ku buryo butunguranye iranga konti yawe muri uyu mukino. Ntifitanye isano na konti iyo ari yo yose ya Epic cyangwa aderesi ya imeri, kandi ihuzwa na konti ya Google gusa iyo uhisemo kwinjira ukoresheje Google (reba hasi).
  • Umukino ukugenera izina ry'umukinnyi, "Player" rikurikiwe n'imibare ine itunguranye (urugero Player4821), ukarioherereza Epic igihe winjira. Ntushobora kurihindura, kandi ntirijya ririmo izina ryawe nyakuri.

Epic ikoresha aya makuru kugira ngo itange serivisi yo kwinjira, guhuza abakinnyi mu mikino n'imiyoboro hagati y'abakinnyi, hakurikijwe Politiki y'ibanga ya Epic Games.

Kwinjira ukoresheje Google (si itegeko)

Ku gice cy'Igenamiterere cy'umukino, ushobora gukanda Injira ukoresheje Google. Si itegeko: umukino, imikino yo kuri murandasi n'iduka byose bikora nta cyo. Intego yacyo yonyine ni ukubika konti yawe y'umukinnyi, bityo n'ikofi yawe, niba wongeye gushyiramo umukino, ugasiba amakuru yawo cyangwa ukimukira kuri telefoni nshya.

  • Android yerekana urupapuro rwa Google rwo kwinjira. Ubwa mbere, Google ikubwira ko izasangiza porogaramu izina ryawe, aderesi ya imeri yawe n'ifoto ya porofayili yawe. Hanyuma Google iha umukino token ya Google ID isinyweho.
  • Umukino wohereza iyo token kuri Epic gusa. Epic irayigenzura, igahuza ID ya konti yawe ya Google na ID yawe y'umukinnyi. Umukino ntusoma kandi ntubika izina ryawe, aderesi ya imeri cyangwa ifoto yawe, kandi seriveri yacu ntijya yakira token cyangwa amakuru ayo ari yo yose ya konti yawe ya Google.
  • Nyuma yo kongera gushyiramo umukino cyangwa kuri telefoni nshya, kwinjira ukoresheje konti ya Google imwe bigarura ID y'umukinnyi imwe n'ikofi imwe. Niba telefoni isanzwe ifite iterambere riri kuri ID y'undi mukinnyi, umukino ukubaza iterambere ugomba kugumana. Epic isiba ID y'umukinnyi utagumanye; ikofi yayo iguma kuri seriveri yacu, aho nta muntu ushobora kuyinjiramo, kugeza udusabye kuyisiba.
  • Umukino ubika dosiye nto kuri telefoni yawe yandika ID y'umukinnyi konti yawe ya Google ihujwe na yo.
  • Sohoka igumana ihuza rya Google kandi igasubiza telefoni kuri konti nshya y'umushyitsi irimo ubusa. Kongera kwinjira bigarura konti yawe.

Igice cy'Igenamiterere kigaragaza kandi kode y'umukinnyi yawe: inyuguti 12 za mbere za ID yawe y'umukinnyi, ushobora gukoporora ukayivuga igihe utwandikiye.

Imikino yo kuri murandasi

Iyo ukanze ngo ukine kuri murandasi, umukino ushakisha icyumba (lobby ya EOS) cy'abakinnyi bagera kuri 10. Igihe uri mu cyumba, umukino utangaza aya makuru kugira ngo imikino y'abandi bakinnyi iyasome:

  • ID yawe y'umukinnyi n'izina ry'umukinnyi, n'intwari wahisemo.
  • Amakuru ya tekiniki akoreshwa mu guhitamo telefoni y'umukinnyi izakira umukino: ingano y'ububiko bw'imbere (memory) bwa telefoni yawe, urwego rw'imikorere y'amashusho yayo, niba iri kuri Wi-Fi cyangwa kuri interineti ya telefoni, niba uburyo bwo kuzigama bateri bufunguye, n'igihe cya ping cyawe ugana ku bandi bakinnyi.
  • Verisiyo y'umukino, n'uko uhuzwa n'umukino bimeze.

Telefoni y'umukinnyi umwe yakira buri mukino wo kuri murandasi, izindi telefoni zikayihuzaho binyuze kuri serivisi ya Epic ya peer-to-peer. Iyo guhuza mu buryo butaziguye bishoboka, telefoni ziri mu mukino zihana amakuru y'urusobe mu buryo butaziguye, bityo buri imwe ishobora kubona aderesi za IP z'izindi. Bitabaye ibyo, amakuru anyura kuri seriveri za Epic zihuza (relay). Mu gihe cy'umukino, umukino w'umwakira wakira ingendo zawe, intwari yawe, imitako wambaye n'ibyavuye mu mukino wawe, kandi woherereza telefoni z'abandi bakinnyi kopi z'uko umukino uhagaze kugira ngo umukino ukomeze niba umwakira avuyemo. Abandi bakinnyi babona izina ryawe ry'umukinnyi mu rutonde rw'abavanywemo no mu byavuye mu mukino.

Umukino nta kiganiro cyanditse cyangwa cy'amajwi ufite, kandi nta rutonde rw'inshuti, ubutumire cyangwa kwerekana ko uri ku murongo ufite.

Ikofi yawe y'iduka (seriveri yacu)

Iduka rigurisha imitako gusa. Kugira ngo amafaranga asigaye adahindurirwa kuri telefoni, ikofi yawe ibikwa kuri seriveri yacu ikandikwa kuri ID yawe y'umukinnyi. Umaze kwinjira muri Epic, umukino woherereza seriveri yacu token yo kwinjira Epic yaguhaye, igaragaza ID yawe y'umukinnyi, maze seriveri yacu igaha umukino isomo (session) rimara iminsi 30. Iyo buri mukino urangiye, umukino w'umwakira (cyangwa umukino wawe bwite, mu mukino ukina wenyine) umenyesha seriveri yacu ID y'umukinnyi wa buri mukinnyi, umwanya yabonye, umubare w'abo yakuyemo n'igihe yamaze akiri muzima, hamwe na ID y'umukino, uburebure bw'umukino n'itariki. Seriveri yacu ibara ibiceri ishingiye kuri iyo raporo, hakurikijwe ibipimo ntarengwa bya buri munsi, ikabyongera mu ikofi ya buri mukinnyi.

Seriveri yacu ntibika izina ryawe ry'umukinnyi, aderesi yawe ya imeri, ubwoko bwa telefoni yawe cyangwa token yawe yo kwinjira muri Epic.

Kugura (Google Play Billing)

Amabuye y'agaciro na paki yo gutangira bigurwa binyuze kuri Google Play Billing. Wishyura Google, kandi amabwiriza ya Google yo kwishyura na Politiki y'Ibanga ya Google ni byo bigenga ubwishyu. Ntitujya tubona cyangwa ngo tubike nimero y'ikarita yawe, amakuru ya banki yawe, aderesi yo kwishyuriraho cyangwa imeri ya konti yawe ya Google. Umukino kandi ubaza Google Play igihugu konti yawe ya Play irimo; igisubizo kiguma kuri telefoni yawe ntikitwoherezwa.

Iyo utangiye kugura, umukino uha Google Play hash y'icyerekezo kimwe (MD5) ya ID yawe y'umukinnyi nk'ikimenyetso cya konti cy'itumiza (icyo Google yita "obfuscated account ID"). Umaze kwishyura, umukino woherereza seriveri yacu igicuruzwa na token yo kugura ya Google Play. Seriveri isaba Google Play kwemeza ko ibyaguzwe ari ukuri, bitigeze bikoreshwa mbere kandi amafaranga yabyo atasubijwe, hanyuma ni bwo yongera amabuye y'agaciro mu ikofi yawe. Tubika inyandiko ya buri kigurwa kugira ngo twemeze ibyaguzwe, turinde uburiganya, dukemure isubizwa ry'amafaranga kandi twuzuze inshingano z'imisoro n'iz'amategeko:

  • Igicuruzwa, nimero y'itumiza rya Google Play, igihe cyo kugura, niba kwari ukugura kw'igerageza, n'uko bihagaze (urugero amafaranga yasubijwe, hamwe n'itariki n'impamvu Google itanga).
  • ID yawe y'umukinnyi, n'ikimenyetso cya konti Google Play itanga ku itumiza (hash ya ID yawe y'umukinnyi).

Inyandiko yandikwa kuri hash y'icyerekezo kimwe (SHA-256) ya token yo kugura. Token ubwayo ntibikwa. Google kandi imenyesha seriveri yacu igihe hari ikiguzwe, amafaranga yasubijwe cyangwa ihagarikwa, kandi seriveri yacu isaba Google urutonde rw'ibyaguzwe byasubijwe amafaranga inshuro nyinshi ku munsi. Seriveri yacu ibika igitabo (log) cy'ayo matangazo, cyandikwa kuri hash imwe na nimero y'itumiza.

Imyaka n'igipimo ntarengwa cy'amafaranga. Kugura ukoresheje amafaranga nyayo ni iby'abakinnyi bafite imyaka 18 cyangwa irenga. Mbere yo kugura bwa mbere ukoresheje amafaranga nyayo, umukino ukubaza niba ufite imyaka 18 cyangwa irenga. Nusubiza ko uri munsi y'imyaka 18, kugura ukoresheje amafaranga nyayo bifungwa kuri iyo telefoni (ibiceri bikomeza gukora). Igisubizo cyawe kibikwa kuri telefoni yawe gusa. Kugura ukoresheje amafaranga nyayo kandi ntibirenza US$200 ku kwezi kwa kalendari (UTC). Mbere y'uko idirishya ryo kugura rya Google Play rifunguka, umukino ugenzura icyo gipimo kuri telefoni yawe, na seriveri yacu ikakigenzura kuri konti yawe, ibara buri kigurwa ku giciro cyacyo cyo muri Amerika.

Iduka nta dusanduku tw'amahirwe (loot boxes) cyangwa ibintu byishyurwa bitoranywa ku buryo butunguranye rifite.

Amakuru abikwa kuri telefoni yawe

Umukino ubika ibi bikurikira mu bubiko bwa porogaramu ubwayo kuri telefoni yawe:

  • Igenamiterere: intwari wahisemo, ubwumva bw'igenzura, kurasa byikora no kubaka ukanze, umubare wa bot, urugero rw'amajwi n'ubwiza bw'amashusho, n'urwego rw'amashusho umukino wamenye ko rukwiye telefoni yawe.
  • Urwego rwawe, amanota y'uburambe, n'umwanya wabonye uheruka n'abo wakuyemo baheruka.
  • Izina ry'umukinnyi wagenewe, na ID y'igikoresho ya Epic (ibikwa na Epic SDK). Niba winjiye ukoresheje Google, ID y'umukinnyi konti yawe ya Google ihujwe na yo.
  • Kopi y'ikofi yawe nk'uko seriveri yacu yayohereje bwa nyuma, kugira ngo iduka rikore nta murandasi; ibyavuye mu mikino n'ibyaguzwe bitegereje koherezwa kuri seriveri yacu; igisubizo cyawe ku kibazo cy'imyaka; n'amafaranga yose yakoreshejwe muri uku kwezi.
  • ID itunguranye y'iri shyirwamo, ikoreshwa gusa n'iduka kuri telefoni mbere y'uko kwinjira muri Epic birangira. Ntijya yoherezwa kuri seriveri yacu.
  • Igihe uri mu mukino wo kuri murandasi, inyandiko ngufi y'icyumba n'umukino (kugira ngo umukino ushobore kongera kwinjira niba ufunze). Isibwa iyo umukino urangiye.
  • Dosiye nto imwe y'imikorere kuri buri somo (umuvuduko w'amashusho n'igenamiterere ry'amashusho by'umukino), ibikwa kuri telefoni yawe kandi ntijya yoherezwa.

Porogaramu yemera Auto Backup ya Android, bityo Android ishobora gushyira aya makuru mu bubiko bw'inkunga bw'igikoresho cyawe kuri konti yawe ya Google, cyangwa ikayakoporora kuri telefoni nshya, bitewe n'igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe. Ubwo bubiko bucungwa na Google, kandi ntidushobora kububona.

Uburenganzira bwa porogaramu

Umukino ntujya ugusaba uburenganzira. Manifeste yawo ya Android itangaza gusa uburenganzira Android itanga itabanje kubaza:

  • Kugera kuri interineti, no kureba uko urusobe na Wi-Fi bihagaze (gukina kuri murandasi, no kumenya niba uri kuri Wi-Fi cyangwa kuri interineti ya telefoni).
  • Google Play Billing, n'igenzura ry'uruhushya rwa Google Play.
  • Kunyeganyega, kurinda telefoni kujya mu gusinzira, no guhindura igenamiterere ry'amajwi (bikoreshwa na moteri y'umukino).
  • Serivisi ikora imbere (guhuza amakuru), no gutangira nyuma y'uko telefoni yongeye gutsa: bikoreshwa n'amasomero (libraries) apakurura kandi agashyiraho amakuru y'umukino avuye kuri Google Play, n'isomero rya Android ry'imirimo ikorerwa inyuma.
  • Uburenganzira bumwe bwihariye bwa porogaramu, bufitwe na porogaramu ubwayo yonyine, bukoreshwa n'amasomero ya Android ayirimo.

Umukino ntusaba uburenganzira bwo kumenya aho uherereye, ku bo muvugana, kamera, mikoro, amafoto cyangwa ububiko, kandi ntushobora kubigeraho. Kwinjira ukoresheje Google (si itegeko) bikoresha Credential Manager ya Android n'isomero rya Google ryo kwinjira, kandi bikora gusa iyo ukanze Injira ukoresheje Google (cyangwa, umaze kwinjira, kugira ngo hagenzurwe ihuza igihe bibaye ngombwa). Paki ya porogaramu irimo kandi amasomero ya serivisi za Google Play moteri y'umukino yongeramo mu buryo busanzwe: isomero rya kera rya Google Sign-In na Play Games Services (byitangiza ubwabyo iyo porogaramu itangiye; ntitwashyizeho umukino wa Play Games, bityo nta kwinjira cyangwa porofayili ya Play Games bikorwa), n'amasomero yo kumenya aho uherereye, Drive na FIDO nta kintu na kimwe mu mukino gihamagara. Nta burenganzira bubijyanye, isomero ryo kumenya aho uherereye ntirishobora kugera aho uherereye.

Amatangazo, isesengura na raporo z'ibibazo

Battle Arena Worlds ntigaragaza amatangazo kandi ntirimo SDK y'amatangazo. Ntirimo serivisi zacu bwite zo gusesengura cyangwa gutanga raporo z'ibibazo, kandi ntigukurikirana mu zindi porogaramu cyangwa imbuga za interineti.

Amakuru dusangiza abandi

Epic Games (Epic Online Services) itanga kwinjira kutazwi nyirako, ibyumba n'imiyoboro hagati y'abakinnyi, kandi yakira amakuru asobanuwe muri "Konti yawe y'umukinnyi" na "Imikino yo kuri murandasi". Niba winjiye ukoresheje Google, Epic yakira kandi token yawe ya Google ID ikabika ihuza riri hagati ya konti yawe ya Google na ID yawe y'umukinnyi. Politiki y'ibanga ya Epic Games.

Google itanga kwinjira ukoresheje Google (si itegeko), bityo niba ukubikoresha, Google imenya ko winjiye muri Battle Arena Worlds. Google Play icunga ubwishyu kandi yakira ikimenyetso cya konti cyahinduwe hash ku matumiza yawe. Seriveri yacu ivugana na Google Play kugira ngo igenzure ibyaguzwe. Politiki y'Ibanga ya Google.

Google Cloud icumbikira seriveri yacu n'ububiko bwacu bw'amakuru. Google ikoresha aya makuru mu izina ryacu. Itangazo ry'ibanga rya Google Cloud.

Abandi bakinnyi bo mu mikino yawe yo kuri murandasi babona izina ryawe ry'umukinnyi, intwari yawe n'imitako wambaye, kandi telefoni zabo zakira amakuru y'umukino asobanuwe muri "Imikino yo kuri murandasi".

Ntitugurisha amakuru yawe, kandi ntituyasangiza undi muntu uwo ari we wese, keretse aho amategeko abisaba.

Aho amakuru yawe abikwa

Seriveri yacu ikorera kuri Google Cloud muri us-central1 (Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika). Umukino utangwa binyuze kuri Google Play mu bihugu byinshi. Aho waba uwukoreshereza hose, amakuru asobanuwe muri "Ikofi yawe y'iduka" na "Kugura" yoherezwa kuri seriveri yacu iri muri Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika, aho amategeko arinda amakuru ashobora gutandukana n'ay'aho uri. Epic ikoresha amakuru yakira nk'uko bisobanuwe muri politiki yayo y'ibanga.

Uko amakuru yawe arindwa

Itumanaho ryose hagati ya porogaramu na seriveri yacu rirasobekwa (encrypted) mu gihe cyo kohererezanya (HTTPS). Seriveri yacu ifungura isomo rya ID y'umukinnyi gusa hakoreshejwe token yo kwinjira yasinywe na Epic, kandi amasomo yayo afitanye isano n'iyi porogaramu na ID yawe y'umukinnyi, akarangira nyuma y'iminsi 30. Igabanya umubare w'ibisabwa yakira, kugira ngo hirindwe ihohoterwa.

Ibitabo (logs) bya seriveri yacu byandika igihe, ubwoko bw'icyasabwe n'icyavuyemo, igihe cyafashe, na aderesi ya IP cyaturutseho. Ibitabo by'ibisabwa bya Google Cloud byandika kandi aderesi yuzuye y'icyasabwe na user agent y'igikoresho cyawe (ishobora kuba irimo verisiyo ya porogaramu n'iya Android). Imirongo imwe y'ibitabo irimo inyuguti 12 za mbere za ID yawe y'umukinnyi, igicuruzwa, nimero y'itumiza rya Google Play, ibintu ugura ukoresheje ibiceri cyangwa amabuye y'agaciro, ibiceri wabariwe nyuma y'umukino, cyangwa inyuguti 12 za mbere za hash ya token yo kugura. Ibitabo ntibijya birimo ID yawe y'umukinnyi yuzuye, token zawe zo kwinjira cyangwa iz'isomo, cyangwa token zo kugura. Tubikoresha mu mutekano, mu kugabanya ihohoterwa no mu gukemura ibibazo. Google Cloud ibibika iminsi igera kuri 30 mu buryo busanzwe.

Igihe tumara tuyabitse

  • Kuri telefoni yawe: kugeza usibye amakuru ya porogaramu cyangwa uyikuyemo. Inyandiko yo kongera kwinjira isibwa iyo buri mukino urangiye.
  • Ikofi yawe, n'inyandiko z'imikino n'ibyaguzwe byamaze kukubarirwa: igihe cyose konti yawe ikiriho, kugeza ubisibye mu mukino cyangwa udusabye kubikora ukoresheje imeri. Ubu nta gusiba kwikora kw'amakofi adakoreshwa guhari. Nitugushyiraho, tuzabivuga hano.
  • Inyandiko z'ibyaguzwe n'iz'amatumiza: zibikwa imyaka 7 nyuma yo kugura, kubera imisoro, isubizwa ry'amafaranga no kurinda uburiganya. Hanyuma igikorwa cyo gusukura gikorwa buri munsi gisiba buri nyandiko, hamwe n'inyandiko y'itumiza ryayo n'amatangazo yayo y'isubizwa cyangwa ihagarikwa.
  • Ibitabo bya seriveri: iminsi igera kuri 30 mu buryo busanzwe, hanyuma bigasibwa mu buryo bwikora na Google Cloud.
  • Epic Online Services: abikwa na Epic hakurikijwe politiki yayo y'ibanga. Ihuza rya Google ribikwa kugeza usibye amakuru yawe mu mukino.

Gusiba amakuru yawe

Mu mukino: ku gice cy'Igenamiterere, munsi ya Konti, kanda Siba amakuru yanjye hanyuma wemeze kabiri (ukenera interineti). Bihita bisiba:

  • kuri seriveri yacu: ikofi yawe (ibiceri, amabuye y'agaciro, imitako ufite n'iyo wambaye, amafaranga yose wakoresheje) n'inyandiko z'imikino n'ibyaguzwe wabariwe. ID yawe y'umukinnyi n'ikimenyetso cya konti cya Google Play bikurwa mu nyandiko z'ibyo waguze, zigakomeza kubikwa zitabifite kubera imisoro, isubizwa ry'amafaranga no kurinda uburiganya (reba "Igihe tumara tuyabitse"). Isomo ryawe rirarangizwa. Ikintu cyaguzwe cyamaze kubarirwa ntigishobora kongera kubarirwa;
  • kuri Epic: ihuza na konti yawe ya Google, niba wararikoze, na ID y'igikoresho, kugira ngo hatagira umuntu wongera kwinjira kuri iyo ID y'umukinnyi;
