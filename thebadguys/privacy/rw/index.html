Itangira gukurikizwa ku wa 18 Ukwakira 2026

The Bad Guys ni umukino w'imirwano wa 3D wa Android aho ukina uri umugome, washyizwe ahagaragara na ST Projects ("twe"), uwakoze porogaramu wigenga ukorera muri Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika. Iyi politiki isobanura amakuru umukino ukusanya, impamvu, n'ibiyabaho. Ireba porogaramu ya Android yashyizwe ahagaragara ku izina rya paki com.stprojects.thebadguys.

Muri make: ntufungura konti iwacu. Aho ugeze bibikwa kuri telefoni yawe. Umukino ntugaragaza amatangazo, kandi kugura binyura kuri Google Play. Niba winjiye muri Google Play Games, aho ugeze n'imigaragarire wahisemo binabikwa kuri seriveri yacu kugira ngo ubashe kubigarura kuri telefoni nshya. Ushobora gusiba ayo makuru yo mu gicu uhereye mu mukino igihe icyo ari cyo cyose. Ushobora gufungura iyi politiki uhereye mu mukino ukoresheje buto ya Politiki y'ibanga iri kuri ecran ya Konti.

Amakuru abikwa ku gikoresho cyawe

Umukino ubika ibi bikurikira mu bubiko bwihariye bwa porogaramu kuri telefoni yawe:

  • Aho ugeze mu mukino: urugendo rwawe rw'ubu (umugome, urwego rw'ingorane, imirimo yakozwe, ibyongerewe imbaraga, itsinda) n'imigaragarire y'abagome wafunguye ukanahitamo.
  • Igenamiterere, nk'ururimi wahisemo no kuba waremeye Amabwiriza ya Serivisi n'iyi politiki.
  • Amakuru y'ibyaguzwe: ibyo waguze n'igihe wabiguriye (amateka y'ibyaguzwe agaragara kuri ecran ya Konti), igiteranyo cy'amafaranga ukoresha buri kwezi, ID z'ibyatumijwe byagejejweho (kugira ngo ikintu kiguzwe kitazigera gitangwa kabiri), n'igisubizo cyawe ku kibazo "imyaka 18 cyangwa irenga" kibazwa mbere yo kugura bwa mbere.
  • Niba ukoresha kubika mu gicu: ID yawe y'umukinnyi ya Google Play Games n'izina rigaragara (rigaragazwa nka "winjiye nka"), n'ikimenyetso cy'umwanya (session token) gituma ukomeza guhuzwa na seriveri yacu.

Aya makuru aguma kuri telefoni yawe keretse ukoresheje kubika mu gicu (hepfo). Porogaramu yemera Auto Backup ya Android, bityo Android ishobora kuyashyira mu bubiko bw'inyongera bw'igikoresho cyawe kuri konti yawe ya Google, cyangwa ikayakoporora kuri telefoni nshya mu gihe cyo kwimura igikoresho, bitewe n'igenamiterere rya telefoni yawe. Ubwo bubiko bw'inyongera bucungwa na Google, kandi ntidushobora kububona.

Umukino ntiwigera ugusaba uruhushya kandi ntushobora kugera aho uri, ku bo muhamagarana, ku mafoto, kuri kamera, kuri mikoro cyangwa ku madosiye yawe. Ukoresha uburenganzira bwo kugera kuri interineti no ku miterere y'umuyoboro, Google Play Billing na Google Play Games. Amasomero ya Google anarimo impushya za wake-lock n'iza serivisi y'imbere (foreground service) ku mirimo ikorerwa inyuma. Umukino ntukubiyemo amatangazo, SDK y'iyamamaza, cyangwa amasomero yacu bwite y'isesengura cyangwa yo gutanga raporo z'ihagarara, kandi ntukoresha ID y'iyamamaza y'igikoresho cyawe.

Kugura (Google Play Billing)

Kugura muri porogaramu bicungwa na Google Play Billing. Wishyura Google, kandi amabwiriza ya Google yo kwishyura na politiki y'ibanga yayo ni yo akurikizwa ku kwishyura. Ntitwigera tubona cyangwa ngo tubike nimero y'ikarita yawe, amakuru ya banki, aderesi yo kwishyuriraho cyangwa imeyili ya konti yawe ya Google.

Umukino ugurisha amapaki atatu y'imigaragarire yitwa "Gaburira Uwakoze Umukino Ushonje". Buri imwe ifungura imigaragarire mishya y'umugome umwe, ishobora kugurwa rimwe kuri buri konti ya Google, kandi ntiyigera irangira.

Iyo uguze ikintu, porogaramu yohereza amakuru y'ibyaguzwe aturuka kuri Google Play (ID y'igicuruzwa, ikimenyetso cy'ibyaguzwe na ID y'ibyatumijwe) kuri seriveri yacu igenzura. Seriveri igenzura na Google Play ko kugura ari ukuri, ko bitakoreshejwe mbere kandi ko amafaranga atasubijwe, hanyuma ni bwo ikintu gitangwa. Tubika inyandiko ya buri kugura (igicuruzwa, ID y'ibyatumijwe, igihe cyo kugura n'imiterere, nko gusubizwa amafaranga) kugira ngo tubashe kwemeza ibyaguzwe, gukumira uburiganya, gucunga isubizwa ry'amafaranga no kubahiriza inshingano z'imisoro n'iz'amategeko. Inyandiko ibikwa munsi ya hash y'icyerekezo kimwe y'ikimenyetso cy'ibyaguzwe. Ikimenyetso ubwacyo ntikibikwa: twakibika gusa ku ifatabuguzi (subscriptions), kandi umukino nta na rimwe ugurisha. Google inamenyesha seriveri yacu iyo amafaranga y'ibyaguzwe asubijwe cyangwa kugura guhagaritswe.

Niba winjiye muri Google Play Games igihe ugura, umukino ushyira ku byaguzwe ikimenyetso cya ID ya konti y'umukinnyi yawe: hash y'icyerekezo kimwe y'izina rya paki ya porogaramu na ID yawe y'umukinnyi ya Play Games, si ID ubwayo. Icyo kimenyetso kijya kuri Google Play hamwe n'ibyatumijwe (nka "ID ya konti ihishe" ya Google) no kuri seriveri yacu hamwe n'igenzura ry'ibyaguzwe. Amapaki y'imigaragarire anahujwe na konti yawe ya Google Play, bityo ushobora kuyagarura utinjiye muri Play Games: kanda Garura ibyaguzwe kuri ecran ya Konti. Niba umukino ugusabye guhitamo hagati y'aho ugeze kuri telefoni yawe n'ibyabitswe mu gicu, amapaki yawe y'imigaragarire aragarurwa uko wahitamo kose.

Kugura ukoresheje amafaranga nyayo bigenewe abakinnyi bafite imyaka 18 cyangwa irenga. Umukino ubaza mbere y'uko ugura bwa mbere kandi ugafunga kugura ku bakinnyi bavuga ko bari munsi yayo. Umukino kandi ugabanya kugura ukoresheje amafaranga nyayo kugeza kuri US$100 mu kwezi kwa kalendari. Ubika igiteranyo kigenda cyiyongera kuri telefoni yawe, nk'igice cy'ibyabitswe by'umukino wawe, kandi imbibi zisubirwamo ku itariki ya 1 ya buri kwezi (UTC).

Amakuru dusangiza abandi

Google Play icunga kwishyura no kwinjira muri Play Games, kandi yakira ikimenyetso cya ID ya konti y'umukinnyi ku byo utumiza winjiye. Seriveri yacu ivugana na Google Play kugira ngo igenzure ibyaguzwe no kwinjira kwawe.

Google Cloud icumbikira seriveri yacu (Cloud Run) n'ububiko bw'amakuru (Firestore). Google itunganya aya makuru mu izina ryacu.

Ntitugurisha amakuru yawe, kandi ntituyasangiza undi uwo ari we wese, keretse aho itegeko ribisaba.

Aho amakuru yawe abikwa

Seriveri yacu n'ububiko bw'amakuru bikorera kuri Google Cloud mu karere ka us-central1, muri Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika. Umukino utangwa binyuze kuri Google Play mu bihugu byinshi. Aho waba uwukoreshereza hose, amakuru asobanuwe munsi ya "Kugura" na "Kubika mu gicu" yoherezwa kandi agatunganyirizwa muri Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika, aho amategeko arinda amakuru ashobora gutandukana n'ay'aho uri. Aho waba utuye hose, iyi politiki irakureba, kandi igice "Uburenganzira bwawe" gisobanura uburenganzira bw'inyongera abantu bo hamwe bafite hakurikijwe amategeko y'iwabo.

Uko amakuru yawe arindwa

Itumanaho ryose hagati ya porogaramu na seriveri yacu rirasobekwa mu gihe cyo kohereza (HTTPS). Umwanya wawe kuri seriveri yacu uhujwe n'iyi porogaramu na ID yawe y'umukinnyi, kandi urangira nyuma y'iminsi 30. Seriveri igabanya umubare w'ibisabwa yakira, kugira ngo ikumire ikoreshwa nabi.

Inyandiko z'ibikorwa (logs) za seriveri yacu zandika igihe, ubwoko bw'icyasabwe n'ibyavuyemo, igihe cyamaze, na aderesi ya IP cyaturutseho. Inyandiko z'ibisabwa za Google Cloud zinandika user agent y'igikoresho cyawe (ubwoko bwa telefoni yawe, verisiyo ya Android na verisiyo ya moteri ya mushakisha). Ku igenzura ry'ibyaguzwe, inyandiko zirimo ID y'igicuruzwa na ID y'ibyatumijwe, kandi amatangazo yo gusubiza amafaranga aturuka kuri Google yandika ID y'ibyatumijwe. Kugira ngo bidufashe gukurikirana ibibazo, imirongo imwe y'inyandiko irimo inyuguti 12 za mbere za ID ya konti y'umukinnyi yawe (kimwe na Kode y'umukinnyi yawe) cyangwa za hash y'ikimenyetso cy'ibyaguzwe. Inyandiko ntizigera zirimo ibyabitswe byawe, ID yawe y'umukinnyi yuzuye cyangwa ID ya konti y'umukinnyi, cyangwa ikimenyetso icyo ari cyo cyose. Tuzikoresha mu bijyanye n'umutekano, gukumira ikoreshwa nabi no gukemura ibibazo. Google Cloud izibika iminsi 30 mu buryo busanzwe.

  • Inyandiko za seriveri: iminsi 30 mu buryo busanzwe, hanyuma zigasibwa mu buryo bwikora na Google Cloud.

Uburenganzira bwawe

Niba uri muri EEA, mu Bwongereza cyangwa mu Busuwisi, itegeko rirengera amakuru (GDPR cyangwa UK GDPR) riguha uburenganzira bwo kubona amakuru bwite tukubikiye, kuyakosora, kuyasibisha, kugabanya cyangwa kwanga uko tuyakoresha, no guhabwa kopi yayo. Ushobora kandi gutanga ikirego ku rwego rushinzwe kurinda amakuru iwanyu. Dukoresha amakuru yawe kugira ngo tuguhe umukino no kubika mu gicu wasabye, ku nyungu zacu zemewe n'amategeko zo gukumira uburiganya no kurinda umutekano wa serivisi, no kubahiriza inshingano z'amategeko nko kubika inyandiko z'imisoro.

Niba uri muri California cyangwa indi Leta ya Amerika ifite itegeko ry'ibanga, ufite uburenganzira bwo kumenya amakuru bwite dukusanya, kuyasibisha cyangwa kuyakosoza, no kwanga ko agurishwa cyangwa asangizwa. Ntitugurisha amakuru bwite, kandi ntituyasangiza ku bw'iyamamaza rishingiye ku myitwarire mu bihe bitandukanye. Ntituzagufata mu buryo butandukanye kubera ko ukoresheje ubu burenganzira.

Kugira ngo ukoreshe ubwo ari bwo bwose muri ubu burenganzira, twandikire kuri aderesi iri hepfo ushyiremo Kode y'umukinnyi igaragara kuri ecran ya Konti, kugira ngo tubone amakuru yawe. Tuzagusubiza mu minsi 30.
