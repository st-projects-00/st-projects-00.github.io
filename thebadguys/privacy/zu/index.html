I-The Bad Guys iyigeyimu yesenzo ye-3D ye-Android lapho udlala khona njengesigebengu, eshicilelwe yi-ST Projects ("thina", "sona"), unjiniyela ozimele ozinze e-United States. Le nqubomgomo ichaza ukuthi yiluphi ulwazi igeyimu eluqoqayo, kungani, nokuthi kwenzekani ngalo. Isebenza ohlelweni lokusebenza lwe-Android olushicilelwe ngaphansi kwegama lephakheji elithi com.stprojects.thebadguys.

Ngamafuphi: awudali i-akhawunti kithi. Inqubekela phambili yakho ilondolozwa efonini yakho. Igeyimu ayibonisi izikhangiso, futhi ukuthenga kudlula nge-Google Play. Uma ungene ku-Google Play Games, inqubekela phambili yakho nokubukeka okukhethile kuphinde kulondolozwe kuseva yethu ukuze ukwazi ukukubuyisa efonini entsha. Ungayisusa leyo datha yefu egeyimini nganoma yisiphi isikhathi. Ungayivula le nqubomgomo egeyimini ngenkinobho ethi Inqubomgomo yobumfihlo esikrinini se-I-akhawunti.

Ulwazi olugcinwe kudivayisi yakho

Igeyimu ilondoloza lokhu okulandelayo endaweni yokugcina yohlelo lokusebenza uqobo efonini yakho:

  • Inqubekela phambili yegeyimu: umzamo wakho wamanje (isigebengu, ubunzima, imisebenzi eyenziwe, ukuthuthukiswa, iqembu) nokubukeka kwezigebengu okuvulile nokukhethile.
  • Izilungiselelo, njengolimi olukhethile nokwamukela kwakho Imigomo Yesevisi nale nqubomgomo.
  • Ulwazi lokuthenga: lokho okuthengile nokuthi nini (umlando wokuthenga oboniswa esikrinini se-I-akhawunti), isamba sakho sokusebenzisa imali senyanga, ama-ID ama-oda okuthenga okulethiwe (ukuze ukuthenga kungalokothi kunikezwe kabili), nempendulo yakho embuzweni othi "18 noma ngaphezulu" obuzwa ngaphambi kokuthenga kokuqala.
  • Uma usebenzisa ukulondoloza efwini: i-ID yakho yomdlali ye-Google Play Games negama lokubonisa (eliboniswa ngokuthi "ungene njenge-"), nethokheni yeseshini ekugcina uxhumene neseva yethu.

Le datha ihlala efonini yakho ngaphandle uma usebenzisa ukulondoloza efwini (ngezansi). Uhlelo lokusebenza luvumela i-Auto Backup ye-Android, ngakho i-Android ingayifaka kusipele sedivayisi yakho ku-akhawunti yakho ye-Google, noma iyikopishele efonini entsha ngesikhathi sokudlulisa idivayisi, kuya ngezilungiselelo zefoni yakho. Leso sipele siphathwa yi-Google, futhi asikwazi ukusibona.

Igeyimu ayilokothi ikucele imvume futhi ayikwazi ukufinyelela endaweni yakho, koxhumana nabo, ezithombeni, kukhamera, kumakrofoni noma kumafayela. Isebenzisa ukufinyelela ku-inthanethi nasesimweni senethiwekhi, i-Google Play Billing ne-Google Play Games. Imitapo yolwazi ye-Google iphinde ibe nezimvume ze-wake-lock nezesevisi engaphambili yemisebenzi yangemuva. Igeyimu ayinazo izikhangiso, ayinayo i-SDK yokukhangisa, futhi ayinayo imitapo yolwazi yethu yokuhlaziya noma yokubika ukuphahlazeka, futhi ayisebenzisi i-ID yokukhangisa yedivayisi yakho.

Ukuthenga (Google Play Billing)

Ukuthenga ngaphakathi kohlelo lokusebenza kuphathwa yi-Google Play Billing. Ukhokhela i-Google, futhi imigomo yokukhokha ye-Google nenqubomgomo yayo yobumfihlo iyasebenza enkokhelweni. Asilokothi sibone noma sigcine inombolo yekhadi lakho, imininingwane yasebhange, ikheli lokukhokha noma i-imeyili ye-akhawunti yakho ye-Google.

Igeyimu ithengisa amaphakethe amathathu okubukeka athi "Yondla Unjiniyela Olambile". Ngalinye livula ukubukeka okusha kwesigebengu esisodwa, lingathengwa kanye nge-akhawunti ngayinye ye-Google, futhi aliphelelwa yisikhathi.

Uma uthenga okuthile, uhlelo lokusebenza luthumela imininingwane yokuthenga evela ku-Google Play (i-ID yomkhiqizo, ithokheni yokuthenga ne-ID ye-oda) kuseva yethu yokuqinisekisa. Iseva ihlola ne-Google Play ukuthi ukuthenga kungokwangempela, akukaze kusetshenziswe ngaphambili futhi imali yakho ayikabuyiselwa, bese kuthi kuphela lapho into inikezwe. Sigcina irekhodi lokuthenga ngakunye (umkhiqizo, i-ID ye-oda, isikhathi sokuthenga nesimo, njengokuthi kubuyiselwe imali) ukuze siqinisekise ukuthenga, sivimbele ukukhwabanisa, siphathe ukubuyiselwa kwemali futhi sifeze izibopho zentela nezomthetho. Irekhodi lifakwa ngaphansi kwe-hash yendlela eyodwa yethokheni yokuthenga. Ithokheni uqobo ayigcinwa: besiyogcina kuphela ekubhaliseni, futhi igeyimu ayithengisi lutho lokho. I-Google iphinde yazise iseva yethu lapho imali yokuthenga ibuyiselwa noma ukuthenga kukhanselwa.

Uma ungene ku-Google Play Games lapho uthenga, igeyimu imaka ukuthenga nge-ID ye-akhawunti yomdlali yakho: i-hash yendlela eyodwa yegama lephakheji lohlelo lokusebenza ne-ID yakho yomdlali ye-Play Games, hhayi i-ID uqobo. Umaki uya ku-Google Play kanye ne-oda (njenge-"ID ye-akhawunti efihliwe" ye-Google) nakuseva yethu kanye nokuhlolwa kokuthenga. Amaphakethe okubukeka nawo aboshelwe ku-akhawunti yakho ye-Google Play, ngakho ungawabuyisa ngaphandle kokungena ku-Play Games: thepha u-Buyisela ukuthenga esikrinini se-I-akhawunti. Uma igeyimu ikucela ukuthi ukhethe phakathi kwenqubekela phambili esefonini yakho nokulondolozwe efwini, amaphakethe akho okubukeka ayabuyiswa noma ngabe ukhetha yikuphi.

Ukuthenga ngemali yangempela kungokwabadlali abaneminyaka engu-18 noma ngaphezulu. Igeyimu iyabuza ngaphambi kokuthenga kwakho kokuqala futhi ivale ukuthenga kubadlali abathi bancane kunalokho. Igeyimu iphinde inciphise ukuthenga ngemali yangempela kube ngu-US$100 ngenyanga yekhalenda. Igcina isamba esiqhubekayo efonini yakho, njengengxenye yokulondoloziwe kwegeyimu yakho, futhi umkhawulo usethwa kabusha mhla lu-1 enyangeni ngayinye (UTC).

Ulwazi olwabelwana nezinkampani zangaphandle

I-Google Play iphatha izinkokhelo nokungena ku-Play Games, futhi ithola umaki we-ID ye-akhawunti yomdlali kuma-oda owenzayo ungene. Iseva yethu ixhumana ne-Google Play ukuze iqinisekise ukuthenga nokungena kwakho.

I-Google Cloud iphethe iseva yethu (Cloud Run) nesizindalwazi (Firestore). I-Google icubungula le datha egameni lethu.

Asithengisi ulwazi lwakho, futhi asabelani ngalo nanoma ubani omunye, ngaphandle uma umthetho ukudinga.

Lapho ulwazi lwakho lugcinwa khona

Iseva yethu nesizindalwazi kusebenza ku-Google Cloud esifundeni se-us-central1, e-United States. Igeyimu inikezwa nge-Google Play emazweni amaningi. Noma ngabe uyisebenzisa kuphi, ulwazi oluchazwe ngaphansi kokuthi "Ukuthenga" nokuthi "Ukulondoloza efwini" luthunyelwa futhi lucutshungulwe e-United States, lapho imithetho yokuvikelwa kwedatha ingahluka kweyalapho ukhona. Noma ngabe uhlalaphi, le nqubomgomo iyasebenza kuwe, futhi isigaba esithi "Amalungelo akho" sichaza amalungelo engeziwe abantu abasezindaweni ezithile abanawo ngaphansi komthetho wendawo.

Indlela ulwazi lwakho oluvikelwa ngayo

Konke ukuxhumana phakathi kohlelo lokusebenza neseva yethu kubethelwa ngesikhathi sokudluliswa (HTTPS). Iseshini yakho neseva yethu iboshelwe kulolu hlelo lokusebenza naku-ID yakho yomdlali, futhi iphelelwa yisikhathi ngemva kwezinsuku ezingu-30. Iseva ikhawulela ukuthi yamukela izicelo ezingaki, ukuze kuvinjelwe ukusetshenziswa kabi.

Amalogi eseva yethu aqopha isikhathi, uhlobo lwesicelo nomphumela waso, ukuthi sathatha isikhathi esingakanani, nekheli le-IP esivela kulo. Amalogi ezicelo e-Google Cloud aphinde aqophe i-user agent yedivayisi yakho (imodeli yefoni yakho, inguqulo ye-Android nenguqulo yenjini yesiphequluli). Ekuhlolweni kokuthenga amalogi afaka i-ID yomkhiqizo ne-ID ye-oda, futhi izaziso zokubuyiselwa kwemali ezivela ku-Google ziloga i-ID ye-oda. Ukusisiza ukulandelela izinkinga, eminye imigqa yelogi ifaka izinhlamvu zokuqala ezingu-12 ze-ID ye-akhawunti yomdlali yakho (efana ne-Ikhodi yomdlali yakho) noma ze-hash yethokheni yokuthenga. Amalogi awalokothi aqukethe okulondoloziwe kwakho, i-ID yakho yomdlali ephelele noma i-ID ye-akhawunti yomdlali, noma yimaphi amathokheni. Siwasebenzisela ukuphepha, ukukhawulela ukusetshenziswa kabi nokulungisa izinkinga. I-Google Cloud iwagcina izinsuku ezingu-30 ngokuzenzakalelayo.

  • Kudivayisi yakho: inqubekela phambili yegeyimu nolwazi lokuthenga kuze uthephe u-Setha kabusha inqubekela phambili (okuphinde kuthathe isikhundla sokulondoloziwe kwakho efwini ngesiqalo esisha uma ungene). I-ID yakho yomdlali ye-Play Games, igama lokubonisa nethokheni yeseshini kuze uthephe u-Susa idatha yefu, usule idatha yohlelo lokusebenza noma ukhiphe uhlelo lokusebenza. Izilungiselelo kuze usule idatha yohlelo lokusebenza noma ukhiphe uhlelo lokusebenza.
  • Okulondolozwe efwini nezipele: kuze ukususe. Okulondoloziwe okusha ngakunye kuthatha isikhundla sokwangaphambili, futhi kugcinwa kuphela izinguqulo ezi-5 zokugcina zangaphambili. Okwamanje akukho ukususwa okuzenzakalelayo kokulondoloziwe okungasebenzi. Uma singakwethula, sizokusho lapha.
  • Amarekhodi okuthenga nama-oda: agcinwa iminyaka engu-7 ngemva kokuthenga, ngenxa yentela, ukubuyiselwa kwemali nokuvimbela ukukhwabanisa. Uma usula idatha yakho yefu, i-ID ye-akhawunti yomdlali yakho isuswa kuwo ngokushesha (bheka "Ukusula idatha yakho" ukuze wazi ukuthi umaki we-oda we-Google ubhalwa kabusha nini), futhi okunye okusele kwerekhodi ngalinye kugcinwa kuze kube yileso sikhathi seminyaka engu-7.
  • Amalogi eseva: izinsuku ezingu-30 ngokuzenzakalelayo, bese esuswa ngokuzenzakalela yi-Google Cloud.

Ukusula idatha yakho

Egeyimini: vula isikrini se-I-akhawunti bese uthepha u-Susa idatha yefu. Lokhu kususa ngokushesha okulondolozwe efwini kwakho, zonke izipele zakho, nerekhodi elixhuma i-ID yakho yomdlali ye-Play Games nedatha yakho, futhi kususa i-ID ye-akhawunti yomdlali yakho kumarekhodi akho okuthenga. Amarekhodi okuthenga uqobo agcinwa kuze kube yiminyaka engu-7 ngemva kokuthenga ngakunye, njengoba kuchaziwe ngenhla, ngaphandle kwe-ID ye-akhawunti yomdlali yakho (ngokuhlukile okukodwa, okuchazwe ngezansi). Inqubekela phambili esefonini yakho iyagcinwa.

Lokhu kusho ukuthini ngokuthenga: amaphakethe akho okubukeka ahlala eboshelwe ku-akhawunti yakho ye-Google Play, ngakho usengawabuyisa ngo-Buyisela ukuthenga.

Ngemva kokusula idatha yakho yefu, ukuthenga kwakho akusaxhunyaniswa nawe, futhi kuhlala kunjalo ngaphandle uma uphinde ungena ku-Play Games. I-Google Play igcina ikhophi yayo yomaki ku-oda ngalinye, futhi asikwazi ukuwususa. Lapho igeyimu iphinde ihlole ukuthenga neseva yethu, iseva ibhala kabusha umaki we-Google (i-ID ye-akhawunti yomdlali yakho) kulelo rekhodi lokuthenga, kodwa ayixhumanisi irekhodi nawe noma ikubuyisele lona. Ngokuvamile igeyimu ihlola amaphakethe akho okubukeka ngale ndlela njalo uma iqala. Uma uphinde ungena ku-Play Games nge-akhawunti efanayo, ukuthenga i-Google esakumaka nge-akhawunti yakho futhi okuphinde kuphathe umaki kurekhodi lethu kungaphinde kuxhunyaniswe nawe. Ukusula idatha yakho yefu akulokothi kuvimbe amaphakethe akho okubukeka ukuthi abuye evela ku-Google Play.

Nge-imeyili: ungasicela futhi ukuthi sisule idatha yakho ngokuthumela i-imeyili ku-battlearenaunlimited@gmail.com. Sicela usho ukuthi kumayelana ne-The Bad Guys futhi ufake Ikhodi yomdlali yakho evela esikrinini se-I-akhawunti. Uma ungakwazi ukuvula igeyimu, thumela esikhundleni salokho inombolo ye-oda (iqala ngo-GPA.) evela kurisidi le-Google Play. Sizophendula phakathi kwezinsuku ezingu-30, futhi sizoqinisekisa uma idatha yakho isisusiwe.

Ukuze ususe inqubekela phambili yakho nolwazi lokuthenga efonini yakho, thepha u-Setha kabusha inqubekela phambili esikrinini se-I-akhawunti (uma ungene, lokhu kuphinde kuthathe isikhundla sokulondolozwe efwini kwakho ngesiqalo esisha). Ukuze ususe konke igeyimu ekugcinayo efonini yakho, okuhlanganisa i-ID yakho yomdlali ye-Play Games, igama lokubonisa, ithokheni yeseshini nezilungiselelo, sula idatha yohlelo lokusebenza kuzilungiselelo ze-Android noma ukhiphe uhlelo lokusebenza. Izinyathelo eziphelele zisekhasini lokusula idatha.

Amalungelo akho

Uma ukwi-EEA, e-UK noma e-Switzerland, umthetho wokuvikelwa kwedatha (GDPR noma UK GDPR) ukunika ilungelo lokufinyelela kudatha yomuntu siqu esiyigcinile ngawe, ukuyilungisa, ukuthi isuswe, ukukhawulela noma ukuphikisa indlela esiyisebenzisa ngayo, nokuthola ikhophi yayo. Ungaphinde ufake isikhalazo kuziphathimandla zokuvikelwa kwedatha zendawo yakini. Sisebenzisa ulwazi lwakho ukuze sinikeze igeyimu nokulondoloza efwini okucelile, ngenxa yezithakazelo zethu ezisemthethweni zokuvimbela ukukhwabanisa nokugcina isevisi iphephile, nokufeza izibopho zomthetho njengokugcina amarekhodi entela.

Uma use-California noma kwesinye isifundazwe sase-US esinomthetho wobumfihlo, unelungelo lokwazi ukuthi yiluphi ulwazi lomuntu siqu esiluqoqayo, ukuthi lususwe noma lulungiswe, nokwenqaba ukuthengiswa kwalo noma ukwabelana ngalo. Asithengisi ulwazi lomuntu siqu, futhi asabelani ngalo ngokukhangisa okusekelwe ekuziphatheni kuzo zonke izimo. Ngeke sikuphathe ngokuhlukile ngokusebenzisa la malungelo.

Ukuze usebenzise noma yiliphi lala malungelo, sithumelele i-imeyili ekhelini elingezansi futhi ufake Ikhodi yomdlali eboniswa esikrinini se-I-akhawunti, ukuze sithole idatha yakho. Sizophendula phakathi kwezinsuku ezingu-30.
