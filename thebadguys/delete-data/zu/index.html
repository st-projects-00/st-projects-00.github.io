I-The Bad Guys ka-ST Projects · iphakheji com.stprojects.thebadguys

I-The Bad Guys ayinawo ama-akhawunti ayo: ukuxhumana kuphela ne-akhawunti ukungena kwakho ku-Google Play Games, futhi leli khasi liyindlela yokusula lokho kuxhumana nedatha yakho. Idatha esiyigcina kuseva yethu exhunyaniswe nawe yi-okulondolozwe efwini kwakho nomaki ekuthengeni okwenzile, okukhona kuphela uma udlale ungene ku-Google Play Games. Ungakusula ngokwakho egeyimini, noma usicele nge-imeyili ukuthi sikususe.

Inketho 1: egeyimini (ngokushesha)

  1. Vula i-The Bad Guys.
  2. Esikrinini sesihloko, thepha u-I-akhawunti.
  3. Thepha u-Susa idatha yefu bese uqinisekisa.

Inkinobho ivela ngenkathi ungene ekulondolozeni efwini. Uma ubona ukuthi "Awungenile", thepha u-Ngena kuqala. Udinga ukuxhumana ne-inthanethi.

Inketho 2: nge-imeyili

Thumela i-imeyili ku-battlearenaunlimited@gmail.com enesihloko esithi "The Bad Guys data deletion". Faka Ikhodi yomdlali eboniswa esikrinini se-I-akhawunti ngenkathi ungene (ibukeka njengo-3FA9-0C21-7B4E), ukuze sithole idatha yakho. Uma ungakwazi ukuvula igeyimu, thumela esikhundleni salokho inombolo ye-oda (iqala ngo-GPA.) evela kurisidi le-Google Play. Sizophendula phakathi kwezinsuku ezingu-30, futhi sizoqinisekisa uma idatha yakho isisusiwe.

Ukuthenga kwakho

Amaphakethe akho okubukeka ahlala eboshelwe ku-akhawunti yakho ye-Google Play, ngakho usengawabuyisa ngo-Buyisela ukuthenga esikrinini se-I-akhawunti.

Ngemva kokusula idatha yakho yefu, ukuthenga kwakho akusaxhunyaniswa nawe, futhi kuhlala kunjalo ngaphandle uma uphinde ungena ku-Play Games. I-Google Play igcina ikhophi yayo yomaki ku-oda ngalinye, futhi asikwazi ukuwususa. Lapho igeyimu iphinde ihlole ukuthenga neseva yethu, iseva ibhala kabusha umaki we-Google (i-ID ye-akhawunti yomdlali yakho) kulelo rekhodi lokuthenga, kodwa ayixhumanisi irekhodi nawe noma ikubuyisele lona. Ngokuvamile igeyimu ihlola amaphakethe akho okubukeka ngale ndlela njalo uma iqala. Uma uphinde ungena ku-Play Games nge-akhawunti efanayo, ukuthenga i-Google esakumaka nge-akhawunti yakho futhi okuphinde kuphathe umaki kurekhodi lethu kungaphinde kuxhunyaniswe nawe. Ukusula idatha yakho yefu akulokothi kuvimbe amaphakethe akho okubukeka ukuthi abuye evela ku-Google Play.

Idatha esefonini yakho

Ukusula idatha yefu akuthinti inqubekela phambili elondolozwe efonini yakho. Nokho kususa i-ID yakho yomdlali ye-Play Games, igama lokubonisa nethokheni yeseshini efonini, futhi ukulondoloza efwini kuhlala kuvaliwe uze uphinde ungene. Ukuze ususe inqubekela phambili yakho nolwazi lokuthenga, thepha u-Setha kabusha inqubekela phambili esikrinini se-I-akhawunti (uma ungene, lokhu kuphinde kuthathe isikhundla sokulondolozwe efwini kwakho ngesiqalo esisha). Ukuze ususe konke igeyimu ekugcinayo efonini yakho, okuhlanganisa izilungiselelo, sula isitoreji sohlelo lokusebenza kuzilungiselelo ze-Android noma ukhiphe uhlelo lokusebenza. Uma ifoni yakho yenza isipele sedatha yezinhlelo zokusebenza ku-akhawunti yakho ye-Google, noma iyikopishe efonini endala ngesikhathi sokudlulisa idivayisi, ungakuphatha lokho kuzilungiselelo zesipele zefoni yakho.
