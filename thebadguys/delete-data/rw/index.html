The Bad Guys ya ST Projects · paki com.stprojects.thebadguys

The Bad Guys nta konti zayo bwite ifite: isano yonyine na konti ni ukwinjira kwawe muri Google Play Games, kandi uru rupapuro rugaragaza uko wasiba iyo sano n'amakuru yayo. Amakuru tubika kuri seriveri yacu ahujwe nawe ni ibyabitswe byawe mu gicu n'ikimenyetso kiri ku byo waguze, biboneka gusa niba warakinnye winjiye muri Google Play Games. Ushobora kubisiba ubwawe mu mukino, cyangwa ukadusaba kuri imeyili ko tubisiba.

Uburyo bwa 1: mu mukino (ako kanya)

  1. Fungura The Bad Guys.
  2. Kuri ecran y'itangiriro, kanda Konti.
  3. Kanda Siba amakuru yo mu gicu hanyuma wemeze.

Buto igaragara mu gihe winjiye mu kubika mu gicu. Niba ubona "Ntiwinjiye", banza ukande Injira. Ukeneye umuyoboro wa interineti.

Ibyo waguze

Amapaki yawe y'imigaragarire aguma ahujwe na konti yawe ya Google Play, bityo uracyashobora kuyagarura ukoresheje Garura ibyaguzwe kuri ecran ya Konti.

Nyuma yo gusiba amakuru yawe yo mu gicu, ibyo waguze ntibiba bigihujwe nawe, kandi bikomeza bityo keretse wongeye kwinjira muri Play Games. Google Play ibika kopi yayo bwite y'ikimenyetso kuri buri byatumijwe, kandi ntidushobora kuyikuraho. Iyo umukino wongeye kugenzura ibyaguzwe na seriveri yacu, seriveri yongera kwandika ikimenyetso cya Google (ID ya konti y'umukinnyi yawe) kuri iyo nyandiko y'ibyaguzwe, ariko ntihuza iyo nyandiko nawe cyangwa ngo iyigusubize. Ubusanzwe umukino ugenzura amapaki yawe y'imigaragarire muri ubu buryo buri gihe utangiye. Niba wongeye kwinjira muri Play Games ukoresheje konti imwe, ibyaguzwe Google igishyiraho ikimenyetso cya konti yawe kandi byongeye kugira ikimenyetso ku nyandiko yacu bishobora kongera guhuzwa nawe. Gusiba amakuru yawe yo mu gicu ntibigera bibuza amapaki yawe y'imigaragarire kugaruka avuye kuri Google Play.

Amakuru ari kuri telefoni yawe

Gusiba amakuru yo mu gicu ntibikora ku byo ugezeho byabitswe kuri telefoni yawe. Ariko bikura kuri telefoni ID yawe y'umukinnyi ya Play Games, izina rigaragara n'ikimenyetso cy'umwanya, kandi kubika mu gicu bikomeza gufunze kugeza wongeye kwinjira. Kugira ngo ukureho aho ugeze n'amakuru y'ibyaguzwe, kanda Siba aho ugeze kuri ecran ya Konti (niba winjiye, ibi binasimbuza ibyabitswe byawe mu gicu intangiriro nshya). Kugira ngo ukureho ibintu byose umukino ubika kuri telefoni yawe, harimo igenamiterere, siba ububiko bwa porogaramu mu igenamiterere rya Android cyangwa ukuremo porogaramu. Niba telefoni yawe ibika kopi y'inyongera y'amakuru ya porogaramu kuri konti yawe ya Google, cyangwa yarayakoporoye kuri telefoni ishaje mu gihe cyo kwimura igikoresho, ushobora kubicunga mu igenamiterere ry'ububiko bw'inyongera bwa telefoni yawe.
