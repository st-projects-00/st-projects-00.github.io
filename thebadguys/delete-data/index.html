Delete your The Bad Guys data

The Bad Guys by ST Projects · package com.stprojects.thebadguys

The Bad Guys has no accounts of its own: the only account link is your Google Play Games sign-in, and this page is how you delete that link and its data. The data we keep on our server that is linked to you is your cloud save and the tag on purchases you made, which exist only if you played while signed in to Google Play Games. You can delete them yourself in the game, or ask us to delete them by email.

Option 1: in the game (immediate)

  1. Open The Bad Guys.
  2. On the title screen, tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete cloud data and confirm.

The button appears while you are signed in to cloud save. If you see "Not signed in", tap Sign in first. You need an internet connection.

Option 2: by email

Email battlearenaunlimited@gmail.com with the subject "The Bad Guys data deletion". Include the Player code shown on the Account screen while you are signed in (it looks like 3FA9-0C21-7B4E), so we can find your data. If you can't open the game, send the order number (it starts with GPA.) from a Google Play receipt instead. We will reply within 30 days, and we will confirm when your data has been deleted.

After you delete your cloud data, cloud save stays off on that phone until you tap Sign in again.

Your purchases

Your look packs stay tied to your Google Play account, so you can still get them back with Restore purchases on the Account screen.

After you delete your cloud data, your purchases are no longer linked to you, and they stay that way unless you sign in to Play Games again. Google Play keeps its own copy of the tag on each order, and we cannot remove it. When the game checks a purchase with our server again, the server writes Google's tag (your player account ID) back onto that purchase record, but it does not link the record to you or restore it to you. The game normally checks your look packs this way each time it starts. If you sign in to Play Games again with the same account, purchases that Google still tags with your account and that carry the tag on our record again can be linked to you again. Deleting your cloud data never stops your look packs coming back from Google Play.

Data on your phone

Deleting cloud data does not touch the progress saved on your phone. It does remove your Play Games player ID, display name and session token from the phone, and cloud save stays off until you sign in again. To remove your progress and purchase information, tap Reset progress on the Account screen (if you are signed in, this also replaces your cloud save with a fresh start). To remove everything the game keeps on your phone, including settings, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. If your phone backs up app data to your Google account, or copied it from an old phone during device transfer, you can manage that in your phone's backup settings.
