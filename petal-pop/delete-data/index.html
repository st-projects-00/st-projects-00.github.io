Delete your Petal Pop data

Petal Pop by ST Projects · package com.stprojects.petalpop

Petal Pop has no accounts of its own. The only data we keep on our server that is linked to you is your cloud save and the tag on purchases you made, which exist only if you played while signed in to Google Play Games. You can delete them yourself in the game, or ask us to delete them by email.

Option 1: in the game (immediate)

  1. Open Petal Pop and open the shop.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Tap Delete cloud data and confirm.

The button appears while you are signed in to cloud save. If you see "Not signed in", tap Sign in first. You need an internet connection.

Option 2: by email

Email battlearenaunlimited@gmail.com with the subject "Petal Pop data deletion". Include the Player code shown on the Account tab while you are signed in (it looks like 3FA9-0C21-7B4E), so we can find your data. If you can't open the game, send the order number (it starts with GPA.) from a Google Play receipt instead. We will reply within 30 days, and we will confirm when your data has been deleted.

What is deleted, what is kept, and for how long

DataWhat happens
Your cloud save and its summary (level, gems, stars, last played, device label)Deleted immediately
The backups of your save (the last 5 earlier versions)Deleted immediately
The record linking your Play Games player ID to your data (with when you first and last signed in)Deleted immediately
Your player account ID on your purchase records (the purchase owner, the ID the game sent with the purchase check, and the order tag we received from Google Play)Removed immediately
Purchase and order records (product, order ID, purchase time, status)Kept for 7 years for tax, refunds and fraud prevention, then deleted. After you delete your cloud data, they no longer carry anything that identifies you.
Server logs (time, request type and result, IP address, device user agent, and for purchase checks the product and order ID)Deleted automatically by Google Cloud after 30 days (the default)

After you delete your cloud data, cloud save stays off on that phone until you tap Sign in again.

Your purchases

Remove ads stays tied to your Google Play account, so you can still get it back with Restore purchases on the Account tab. The days left on a Monthly card can no longer be restored on another phone from our purchase records. They stay in the save on the phone you are using (and if you sign in again later, that save is uploaded to a new cloud save). Everything else you bought was added to your game when you bought it.

Google Play keeps its own copy of the tag on each order, and we cannot remove it. If your phone later sends one of those purchases to our server to be checked again, that purchase record is linked to your player account ID again.

Data on your phone

Deleting cloud data does not touch the progress saved on your phone. To remove that too, tap Reset progress on the Account tab, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. If your phone backs up app data to your Google account, or copied it from an old phone during device transfer, you can manage that in your phone's backup settings.

Ad data

Information collected by Google AdMob to show ads is held by Google, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, and learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services.
