Lantern Lane Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026

Lantern Lane is a hidden-object detective mystery game for Android, published by ST Projects ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and what happens to it. It applies to the Android app published under the package name com.stprojects.lanternlane.

In short: you don't create an account with us. Your progress is saved on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and purchases go through Google Play. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your progress is also saved on our server so you can get it back on a new phone. You can delete that cloud data from the game at any time.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in the app's own storage on your phone:

  • Game progress: scenes opened and solved, mastery stars and clues, lamps lit on the Lane, coins, gems, lamp oil, hints and other tools, star boxes, the daily case, event and league progress, and Casebook progress.
  • Settings, such as the light or dark theme.
  • Purchase information: what you bought and when (the purchase history shown on the Account tab), your monthly spending total, the order IDs of delivered purchases (so a purchase is never granted twice), and your answer to the "18 or older" question asked before a first purchase.
  • If you use cloud save: your Google Play Games player ID and display name (shown as "signed in as"), and a session token that keeps you connected to our server.

This data stays on your phone unless you use cloud save (below). The app allows Android's Auto Backup, so Android may include it in your device backup to your Google account, depending on your phone's backup settings. That backup is handled by Google, and we cannot see it.

The game does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files. Its only Android permission is internet access. It contains no analytics or crash-reporting libraries of our own.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Lantern Lane shows ads served by Google AdMob:

  • Reward videos, which you choose to watch for an in-game reward, for example 30 more seconds on the clock, a free hint, extra lamp oil or cheaper scenes for a while. They are always optional and limited to 8 a day.
  • Interstitial ads, shown between scenes (at most one every 2 scenes). If you buy Remove ads, interstitial ads stop. Reward videos stay available if you want them.

To show ads, measure them and prevent ad fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects information directly from your device and sends it to Google. This can include your device's advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be worked out), information about your device and the app, and your interactions with ads. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices. When the game starts, it uses Google's consent tool (the User Messaging Platform) to check whether consent is needed where you are. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and anywhere else Google's tool requires it, you are shown Google's consent message and can choose whether ads are personalised. You can change your choice later with the Ad privacy choices button on the Account tab of the shop, which appears when your region requires it. On any Android phone you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. You pay Google, and Google's payment terms and privacy policy apply to the payment. We never see or store your card number, bank details, billing address or Google account email.

When you buy something, the app sends the purchase details from Google Play (the product ID, the purchase token and the order ID) to our verification server. The server checks with Google Play that the purchase is genuine, has not been used before and has not been refunded, and only then is the item granted. We keep a record of each purchase (product, order ID, purchase time and status, such as refunded) so we can confirm purchases, prevent fraud, handle refunds and meet tax and legal obligations. The purchase token itself is stored only as a one-way hash. Google also tells our server when a purchase is refunded or cancelled.

If you are signed in to Google Play Games when you buy, the purchase is tagged with a one-way hash of your player ID, so that permanent purchases like Remove ads and the Night watch card can be restored on another phone.

Real-money purchases are for players 18 or older. The game asks before your first purchase and turns purchases off for players who say they are younger.

Cloud save (optional, through Google Play Games)

You don't need to sign in to play. If your phone is signed in to Google Play Games, the game connects to it automatically and saves your progress on our server, so you can restore it after reinstalling or on a new phone. If you are not signed in, none of the information in this section is sent.

Our server does not store your Play Games display name, your email address, or your Google sign-in tokens. The game asks Google for no extra permissions beyond Play Games sign-in. Google's handling of your Play Games account is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

To stop cloud save, use Delete cloud data (see below). After that, cloud save stays off on that phone until you tap Sign in on the Account tab.

Information shared with third parties

Google AdMob collects information directly through the app to show ads, as described above.

Google Play handles payments and Play Games sign-in, and our server contacts Google Play to verify purchases and your sign-in.

Google Cloud hosts our server (Cloud Run) and database (Firestore). Google processes this data on our behalf.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Where your information is stored

Our server and database run on Google Cloud in the us-central1 region, in the United States. If you live outside the United States, the information described under "Purchases" and "Cloud save" is transferred to and processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those where you live.

How your information is protected

All communication between the app and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your session with our server is tied to this app and your player ID, and it expires after 30 days. Our server's logs record the time, the type of request and the IP address it came from, for security and to limit abuse, but they do not record your save, your player ID or any tokens. Google Cloud keeps these logs for a limited time (30 days by default).

How long we keep it

  • On your device: until you tap Reset progress, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud save and backups: until you delete them. Each new save replaces the previous one, and only the last 5 earlier versions are kept. There is currently no automatic deletion of inactive saves. If we introduce one, we will say so here.
  • Purchase and order records: kept after you delete your cloud data, because we need them for refunds, fraud prevention, tax and legal reasons. When you delete your cloud data, they are unlinked from you. If you later restore a purchase that Google still reports as yours, it is linked to you again.

Deleting your data

In the game: open the shop, go to the Account tab and tap Delete cloud data. This immediately deletes your cloud save, all its backups and the link between your Play Games player ID and your data. Progress on your phone is kept. Your purchases stay tied to your Google Play account, so you can still restore them.

By email: you can also ask us to delete your data by emailing battlearenaunlimited@gmail.com. Please say it is about Lantern Lane. We will delete it and confirm by reply.

To remove the data on your phone, tap Reset progress on the Account tab, or uninstall the app. The full steps are on the data deletion page.

Your rights

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, data protection law (GDPR or UK GDPR) gives you the right to access the personal data we hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, restrict or object to how we use it, and receive a copy of it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. We use your information to provide the game and cloud save you asked for, for our legitimate interests in preventing fraud and keeping the service secure, to meet legal obligations such as keeping tax records, and, for personalised ads, on the basis of the consent you give in Google's consent message.

If you are in California or another US state with a privacy law, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to have it deleted or corrected, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information. Apart from the information Google's AdMob SDK collects to show ads, as described above, we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can limit ad personalisation with the Ad privacy choices button where it is shown, and by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your phone's settings. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

To use any of these rights, email us at the address below. We may need to ask for details, such as your Play Games player name, to find your data.

Children

Lantern Lane is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used cloud save, contact us at the address below and we will delete the data.
