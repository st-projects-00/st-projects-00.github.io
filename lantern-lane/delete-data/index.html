Delete your Lantern Lane data

Lantern Lane by ST Projects · package com.stprojects.lanternlane

Lantern Lane has no accounts of its own. The only data we keep on our server is your cloud save, which exists only if you played while signed in to Google Play Games. You can delete it yourself in the game, or ask us to delete it by email.

Option 1: in the game (immediate)

  1. Open Lantern Lane and open the shop.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Tap Delete cloud data and confirm.

The button appears while you are signed in to cloud save. If you see "Not signed in", tap Sign in first. You need an internet connection.

Option 2: by email

Email battlearenaunlimited@gmail.com with the subject "Lantern Lane data deletion". Include your Google Play Games player name so we can find your save. We will delete it and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your cloud save and its summary (scenes solved, stars, coins, gems, last played, device label)Deleted immediately
The backups of your save (the last 5 earlier versions)Deleted immediately
The link between your Play Games player ID and your dataDeleted immediately
Purchase and order recordsKept, but unlinked from your player ID. We need them for refunds, fraud prevention, tax and legal reasons. Your purchases stay tied to your Google Play account, so you can still restore permanent ones like Remove ads.
Server request logs (time, request type, IP address)Removed automatically by Google Cloud after a limited time (30 days by default)

After you delete your cloud data, cloud save stays off on that phone until you tap Sign in again.

Data on your phone

Deleting cloud data does not touch the progress saved on your phone. To remove that too, tap Reset progress on the Account tab, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. If your phone backs up app data to your Google account, you can manage that backup in your phone's backup settings.

Ad data

Information collected by Google AdMob to show ads is held by Google, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, and learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services.
